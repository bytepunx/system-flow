## Decisions

- **Plan, revised from the planner's five single-task layers.**
  - Layer 1: T-1216 (ADR and metrics.md) and T-1217 (conflict log). They share no path, and the definitions below give T-1217 its record shape, so it need not wait for the ADR text.
  - Layer 2: T-1218 (flai stats). It waits for T-1216, whose definitions it computes, and T-1217, whose log reader it calls.
  - Layer 3: T-1219 (chart) and T-1220 (docs). Both wait for T-1218's JSON shape and share no path. T-1220 no longer waits for T-1219: it documents the chart from the definition below, not from its code.
- **Conversations are counted per entry, by the ISO week of the entry's heading time.** A conversation has no kind and can mix notices, and `updated` moves with every entry. The heading time is when the event happened.
  - `opened`: the conversation's first entry.
  - `agreed`: the first of a share in `shares[]` or a closing entry for a clean merge at sync. Each conversation is counted once.
  - `escalated`: the first escalation entry. Each conversation is counted once.
  - `closed_with_story`: a closing entry saying a story was accepted, archived, or no longer open. For a conversation read as closed with no closing entry, the time is the first done or cancelled transition of either story.
  - Not counted: a narrowed claim and agreement in free text. Nothing records either against the conversation, and the ADR says so.
- **Conflicts come from a new `.flai-cache/conflicts.jsonl` in the main checkout's cache, beside `overlaps.jsonl`.** It holds one line per event: `at`, `story`, `other` (trial merges only), `where` (`trial`, `sync`, or `accept`), and `paths`.
  - It is append-only and never trimmed. A failed write is logged as a warning and the command goes on, unlike `overlaps.jsonl`, which drops failures silently and trims to 500.
  - A trial-merge line is written only when the trial merge sends or changes a conflict message. The conversation already dedups by text, so a pair counts once per distinct conflict, not once per sync.
  - A rebase stop is written at `storygit.Rebase` for `sync`, which `flai task done` reaches too, and in `flai accept`'s merge before it refuses, for `accept`.
  - Generated files the sync settles itself (ADR-0098) are left out of `paths`. A stop settled by them alone writes no line.
  - A missing log means no conflicts. An unreadable log is warned, and `conflicts` is left out of the weeks.
- **Shares.** A story started on a share is a story whose first move to in-progress falls while a share in some conversation names it as `held`: the share's `at` is at or before the move, and the holder has not yet left in-progress. Its week is that move's week.
  - The hold it would have waited runs from that move to the holder's first move to review, done, or cancelled after it, or to now while the holder is still in progress.
  - This is the counterpart of ADR-0113's `held_seconds`. The existing hold replay already ignores conversation shares.
- **JSON shape, under `coordination`.**
  - `logged`: whether the conflict log was readable.
  - `weeks[]`: keyed as `claims.weeks[]` is, each with:
    - `conversations{opened, agreed, escalated, closed_with_story}`
    - `conflicts{found, found_paths, met_sync, met_sync_paths, met_accept, met_accept_paths}`, absent when the log is unreadable
    - `shares{started, held_seconds}`
