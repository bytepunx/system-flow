# S-0201 The story page shows [Draft] and finalizes a draft story, and cards mark drafts

## Context

S-0199 (ADR-0074) gave stories a `draft` flag, cleared by `flai edit --no-draft` or `flai move <story> ready --yes`, and the dashboard's `item.move` `finalize`. This story shows drafts in the dashboard and adds a Finalize action. The edit form does not change status (the item page's move buttons do), so its criterion is read as: the form warns that a draft cannot go to ready and offers Finalize.

## Decisions

- Plan, three layers. Layer 1: T-0753 (Go: board cards and search hits carry `draft`) and T-0754 (story page indicator, Finalize button, `/api/items/:id/finalize` route); no path in common. Layer 2: T-0755 (cards, overview, search marker) after T-0753 for its data; T-0756 (record who finalized, hostapi `item.finalize`) after T-0753 because both touch `flai/internal/workitem`, and after TH-0083; T-0757 (edit form) after T-0754, whose route it posts to. Layer 3: T-0758 (docs, ADR) after all four, which it describes.
- T-0753 also changed `flai/internal/hostapi/searching.go`, outside its touches: the host's search index builds hits from front matter and had to read `draft`. Its touches were widened with `flai edit`.
- Finalize is its own hostapi write, `item.finalize`, as the criterion names it, rather than `item.edit` `draft: false`: a one-purpose op the card menu (S-0202) can reuse.
- T-0756 (on TH-0083's recommendation): a `finalized: {by, at}` block on a story, stamped by `flai edit --no-draft` and by a finalizing move; `flai edit --draft` removes it; a story still a draft with the block is invalid. ADR-0077 records it, accepted after TH-0083's answer.
- `item.finalize` refuses a story that is not a draft as a rule, by reading `flai edit`'s `unchanged` answer through a new `judge` hook on hostapi specs: `flai edit --no-draft` on a non-draft stays a no-op, as S-0199 tested it.
- The edit form takes the item's new hash after a finalize only when its words are unchanged, so a later save still reports a real conflict.
- The overview lists counts, not items, so it has no marker; "the items list" in the criterion is the epic page's list of its stories, marked by T-0754.
- MCP `item_get` does not return `finalized` yet: `flai/internal/mcpserver` is S-0200's to change while it is in progress. `flai show --json` returns it.
- The ADR was made as 0075 and renumbered to 0077 at the rebase, after S-0207's ADR-0075 and S-0200's 0076 (I-0063).
- This story's issue was renumbered I-0062 to I-0063 by hand: there is no `flai issue` renumber command, and S-0207's I-0062 was already on main.
- 2026-10-03: TH-0083: how finalizing records who did it. Recommended a `finalized: {by, at}` block on the story. — take the proposed recommendation in the thread (answered by alex)
