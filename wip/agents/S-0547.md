## Decisions

- The planner's six tasks are kept as drafted; the orchestrator approved them on TH-0774.
- Layer 1: T-2375 (the ADR), T-2376 (the `when` key), and T-2377 (`{importers}` on go-test). They wait for nothing and share no path, so they run together.
- Layer 2: T-2378 (verify runs a queue tier whose `when` matches). It waits for T-2376, whose `Tier.When` it reads.
- Layer 3: T-2379 (`when` on integration and smoke, proof run, I-0221) waits for T-2377 and T-2378: it shares their tier list and needs `when` to choose. T-2380 (docs) waits for T-2375 and T-2378: it links the ADR and describes the selection.
- Criterion 1 is met through `{importers}` on this repository's go-test tier, not by changing what `{packages}` means for every project (TH-0774).
- go-test names `-timeout 20m`, the flag and place S-0548 uses. With `{importers}`, a change to verify runs nearly the whole module under `-race`, which ran mcpserver into the 10-minute default twice (I-0227). The identical flag keeps the rebase over S-0548 to its `-short` (MS-0257).
- The claim that `when` matches comes from the project in `verify.Verify` itself (`Holds.Claim`), not from new options that `flai/cmd/verify.go` and `flai/internal/mcpserver/verify.go` would set. This keeps out of the call sites S-0546 rewrites (MS-0268).
- `when` on integration and smoke is `["flai/**", "template/**"]`, as planned. It is hand-edited in `system-flow.yaml`: `flai manifest set tests=` wrote the same tiers but requoted four unrelated `paths` lines, which would add conflicts with S-0548. `flai check` validates the hand edit.
- T-2379's proof run is the story's close-out, not a separate `flai verify` before it. A second full run of integration and smoke would cost about 12 minutes and be stale after the next commit. I-0221's closing reason names the close-out recorded in the story's notes. If the close-out fails, it is fixed and run again before review.
- The close-out runs integration and smoke, longer than the Bash tool's 10-minute limit, so it runs as a background Bash command with a longer timeout. S-0546's serve job is not on main yet.
- Splits: S-0548 (MS-0257) keeps `-timeout 20m` and `-short` on the go-test line and its by-name tier; S-0552 (MS-0262) keeps accept, move, queue, and stats in `flai/cmd`; S-0546 (MS-0268) keeps the verify record, `--job`, and the MCP job request.
