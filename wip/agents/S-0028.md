## Decisions
- One runbook page per operation (six pages), each with a section for flai and one for flaiover, rather than twelve pages: the two share most steps (flai starts and stops flaiover), so splitting them would duplicate text. A reading of criterion 2.
- The flag index is generated by `flai reference` from the command tree, not written by hand: about two hundred flag rows would drift at the first new flag. The hand-written tables are kept complete by a test that reads the config and manifest schemas and flai's source for variable names.
- `settings.md` is an index: a phrase and a link per setting. The explanations stay where they are (`docs/users/flai.md`, `docs/operators/index.md`) rather than moving, because S-0115 and S-0116 are editing those pages now and a move would duplicate or conflict.
- The story is tagged docs and operators and touches `flai/cmd` for the reference generator and tests; flai gets an incidental patch when it publishes.
