- T-2679 left `coordination.go` calling `stateAt`: it calls it once for each share of a held story, at the moment the story moved, not once a day, so it is not the per-day parse cause 18 measured; its signature is what `coordination_test.go` calls. The day sections still read every item each day from the first item's day, by a cursor with nothing to parse, rather than adding into difference arrays, so the sums keep the order that makes the report byte for byte the same.
- T-2675's `planning.Puller` starts each order from fresh lanes and shares only the forecaster's estimates, which depend on the item alone. T-2672 keeps a thread's parse while its body is the same string, rather than once per thread, since callers assign `Body`.
- T-2683 keys the hold timeline by the root, the item generation, and the manifest's projects and shared paths, but not the commits' key: the replay reads no commit, and branch tips move far more often than items. The touches drift takes the commits' key too. A kept timeline answers only a now at or after its last applied event and before any event it left out; otherwise the request replays and replaces it.
