## Decisions

T-3251's touches are catalog.ts, charts.ts, explain.ts, their tests, the page, and its test. stats.ts and routes/charts/+page.ts need no change, since stats.ts hands flai's answer through whole.
- The state charts stack the board's lanes in order and colour each with workflow.ts's stateSlot (S-0758). Cumulative flow, time in state, and the share leave out lanes of kind cancelled. Cost of delay stacks the open lanes, those neither accepted nor cancelled. On the baseline that gives today's STATES and COD_COLUMNS exactly. With no lanes known, as from an older flai, they stack the states the report carries.
- A workflow's own timer's chart is the kind `timer-<name>`, at /charts/timer-<name>. Cycle Time is the same builder over the `cycle` timer's fields. The Charts page links the timer charts from Cycle Time and from each timer chart. The rail keeps its fixed catalogue: listing the timers there needs a stats call on every page load. The Charts page is where criterion 2's catalogue entry appears.
- No new ADR: a row in metrics.md § Charts for every chart is ADR-0219's own rule, and the timers' definitions are ADR-0317's.
