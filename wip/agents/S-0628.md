## Decisions

- Remedy A, as the planner drafted it: a container story's close-out job runs `flai verify --record-issues` in the story's container, the commit and the sync run on the host through flai's own code with the main checkout's hooks, and the operator's checks for such a story run in the container. The operator finalized S-0628 with A's tasks, and ADR-0253 already says the close-out runs inside the container. TH-0949's recommendation on the cost of delay and the remedy still awaits the operator; nothing here waits on the cost of delay.
- ADR-0272 is taken (the checked-write lock). T-2772's ADR takes the next number `adr_new` gives, and T-2784 and T-2785 link that number.
- The symlink check in `flai/internal/docedit/file.go` stays out of scope, as the planner and the orchestrator recommended.
- Plan, reviewed and kept as the planner drafted it:
  - Layer 1: T-2772 (the ADR) and T-2775 (the container adapter's argv for one command). No after, no shared path: they run together.
  - Layer 2: T-2777 (the close-out job). It runs T-2775's argv.
  - Layer 3: T-2782 (the operator's checks). It runs T-2775's argv in the image T-2777 exposes.
  - Layer 4: T-2784 (the documents). It describes T-2772's decision and T-2777's and T-2782's behaviour.
  - Layer 5: T-2785 (close I-0295). Its reason names what every other task did.
