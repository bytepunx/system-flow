## Decisions
- 2026-09-20: search is hand-written in Go (`internal/search`, about 250 lines) with MiniSearch's fields, boosts, and match weights, and BM25+; no library. Reasons and the comparison are in `design/tech/search.md`. One deliberate difference: a one-character prefix is not followed, which is why an item ID now finds its item.
- 2026-09-20: the index is rebuilt when a stat-only walk shows the files changed, not on the watcher's word, so the method table stays stateless towards the watcher and an index cannot be stale.
- 2026-09-20: nothing is paged. The criterion allowed either; the largest answer on this repository is 623 KiB against a 16 MiB cap, and an answer over the cap is an explicit error.
- 2026-09-20: overlaps in the inbox come from `check.Run`, the rule's one implementation, as the TypeScript did by running `flai check`.
- 2026-09-20: a commit went in with a lint finding once in this story (the gate was a pipe to tail); fixed in the next commit, and the later commits are gated on the log's content.
- 2026-09-20: based on `story/S-0073` (in review) by fast-forward; rebased if that story is sent back.
- 2026-09-20: flai answers data, the dashboard builds its own routes: inbox entries and search hits carry the item or path, and `href` and `route` are made in TypeScript. Inbox keys keep the TypeScript hash (djb2 over UTF-16 units, base 36) so that nothing becomes new to the notifier by the move.
