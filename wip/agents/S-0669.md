## Decisions

- **Cause.** The page I-0278 calls "the story's agent page" is the Activity page, not the story page. TH-0836 asked the operator to "press Stop on S-0556's activity page" (its recommendations before the stop). agent-S-0565 compared the operator's answer with ADR-0058 there: "§4 and the activity page's Stop (§1) suggest Retry beside it on that page." `ActivityView.svelte` draws Stop under `stoppable()` and never draws Retry. After the stop its card reads `agent stopped by the operator (…)` and offers nothing to press.
- **Evidence.** flai's `runActivity` (`flai/internal/serve/agents_activity.go`) reports a stopped run as `failed`, the same to the board and to every page. The story page's `StoryAgent.svelte` and the card's menu (`cardmenu.ts`) both pass the board's own inputs to `retryable()`: the story's status, `writable` from `/api/board`, and `enabled` from `/api/host-agent`. So both offer Retry for a stopped run of a story in progress. Only `ActivityView` lacks it.
- **Fix.** The Activity page offers Retry on a story's activity line under `retryable()`, the rule the story page and the card's menu share (S-0202). It needs `writable`, which the page asks `/api/board` for, as the item page does. This is what ADR-0058 §4 already says the dashboard shows, so no thread is needed.
- **Replan.** The planner aimed T-2907 and T-2909 at `StoryAgent.svelte` and the item page, from I-0278's guess. I retargeted both, and the story's touches, to `ActivityView.svelte`, its test, `routes/activity/+page.svelte`, `activity.test.ts`, and `design/system/flaiover-dashboard.md`. `StoryAgent` and the item page are left alone: they already offer Retry.
- **Layers.** These are the planner's, kept: T-2907 (the failing test), then T-2909 (the fix, against that test), then T-2912 (closing I-0278 with the fix named). Each waits for the one before. T-2909 makes T-2907's test pass, and T-2912 names T-2909's change. No two of them run together.
- **T-3072, added at close-out.** Two close-outs stopped at the flaiover tier on one test outside the story: `routes/api/agent/agent.test.ts` "does not ask a candidate for project.info" timed out at vitest's 5 s default. 2431 of 2432 tests passed, and the host's load average was 60 to 90. I recorded this as I-0341 and bumped it on the second stop. The test's first case pays the cold import of `./+server` after `vi.resetModules()`. This was a five-minute fix, so I added it as a task on this story (`work-management.md`): the describe takes `{ timeout: 30000 }`, as `search.test.ts`'s monorepo describe does. I-0341 is closed with that fix. No deterministic test reproduces load (D-0550).
