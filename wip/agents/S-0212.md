## Decisions

- Kept the planner's tasks as written. They share `flaiover/src/lib/viz/charts.ts` and its test, so the three chart builders run one after another.
- Added T-1156 and ADR-0111: the per-item stats carried no agent model, so the dashboard could not filter by model or work out a model's p50 per bucket to match `forecasts.*.by_model`. Taking the model from `usage.models` would count sub-agents' models and disagree with flai. The planner's note that the story changes no flai was wrong on this point.
- Layers: 1) T-1156 (flai, done by me, small) and T-0950 (no shared path); 2) T-0951 (after T-0950, same files); 3) T-0956 (after T-0951, same files); 4) T-0958 (page and table, needs all three builders) with T-0962 (docs only, no shared path). T-1156 ran before T-0950 rather than beside it because it was a few minutes' work.
- Task sub-agents: T-0950 drew the p50 and p85 lines at plus and minus their value, since the axis is the signed error, and left the estimate series without lines; points take done stories only (`doneIn`), as flai's `forecasts` do. T-0951 plots delivery error in exact days (seconds over 86400) on a second y axis beside the weekly on-time share, the one chart with two. T-0956 takes the bucket from the report's `usage.bucket`, as the spend charts do, and leaves an empty bucket a gap. T-0958 always asks `/api/stats` for stories on the planning charts, and its strip shows flai's figures under no or one filter and works them out under both; it added a notice for a flai too old to send `forecasts`.
