## Decisions

- The planner's plan is kept: eight tasks in five layers. Layer 1 is T-2301 (the three manifest keys). Layer 2 is T-2300 (the trigger, which names `orchestration.schedule`) and T-2302 (the queue, which reads `orchestration.coalesce`); both wait for T-2301 and share no path. Layer 3 is T-2303 (the prompt), T-2304 (the end), and T-2305 (the timeout); each waits for T-2302's `Request.Reasons` and run shape, and they share no path. Layer 4 is T-2306 (the docs), after the behaviour is settled. Layer 5 is T-2307.
- `design/adrs` is dropped from the story's touches (TH-0717, proposal 1). No task writes an ADR, and a story's agent may narrow its own claim (work-management.md).
- T-2302 gains `flai/internal/inbox/inbox.go` and its test. flai serve takes the orchestrator's reasons from `inbox.CatchUp` under a cursor of its own, reading as the orchestrator. That leaves out the orchestrator's own changes and judges whom a thread awaits, without advancing the orchestrator's own inbox cursor.
- Reasons go to a run when it starts. Reasons that arrive during the run start the next one (ADR-0209 §2). So "nothing queued remains for it" (§4) means the run's own reasons, and `wait_for_events` ends any orchestrator run flai serve started with reasons. An orchestrator run by hand, with no reasons, keeps holding.
- When orchestrate is turned on, or at flai serve's first look, one `asked` reason is queued. Events from before that are not queued, as the curator does not act on the past (ADR-0199).
- T-2307 and the second half of criterion 6 wait on the operator's confirmation of TH-0717's recommendation.
- T-2302 merged onto S-0534's budget hold at its sync: `launcher.orchestrate` answers why it was refused, the budget state that held it, and whether it started. The coalesced start goes through `spawnGated`, and reasons stay queued while the state holds (ADR-0209 §5).
- A run keeps its reasons as `taken` until it ends. A failed run, on the usage limit or otherwise, gives them back ahead of those queued since. T-2306's sub-agent found that a start dropped them for good, and §5 says they wait. The fix is committed under T-2302.
- `flai serve orchestrate start` now starts a run that is neither held nor live, since runs end on their own. It is refused only while a run is going (T-2302).
- The `.claude/agents/orchestrator.md` pair moved from T-2303 to its own task, T-2330. T-2303's code and role files could then close while the protected write waits on TH-0737.
- `docs/operators/index.md` is shared with S-0515 (MS-0230): S-0536 changes the orchestrator's sections, S-0515 the HTTP MCP server's. `design/system/flai-cli.md` conflicts with S-0515 on a trial merge. S-0515 is in review, so S-0536 resolves the conflict at its sync after that acceptance, keeping both changes.
