- The planner's five tasks stand as drafted. - Shared paths: in `harness.go` and `harness_test.go` only `planPrompt`, a `Request` field for the thread, and their tests are S-0524's; S-0419 keeps `Builtin` and its test, and S-0523 keeps the cost of delay rules of `orchestratePrompt`. Resolved threads count, as ADR-0222 does not limit them to open ones. I moved the `OperatorName` check out of the per-item loop, reworded the help text, and regenerated `docs/users/flai-reference.md` in the same task, adding it to T-2247's touches so `TestReferenceIsCurrent` stays green between tasks.
