ADR-0301 §4 puts the rules in workflow/rules.go; the split keeps workflow free of work items, as its package comment says.

- T-3197 shrinks to its test: S-0737 already wrote the rules on the baseline's transitions as ADR-0301 §3 lists them, so the task adds baseline_test.go's check that each transition lists them and that each parses under the vocabulary.
- T-3202: a rule's check gives a refusal text and a warning text, alike for all but criteria-ticked, which keeps today's two (refuse at done, warn at review); a transition's severity picks one. A worktree git cannot read always warns. kluster-verified's check in workitem finds nothing: flai move and item_move check it through mcpserver.ReviewWarnings, which read the namespace's record, and moving that onto the transition's rules is S-0744's (cmd/move).
- Accepted as it is: a rule an operator's workflow puts on another transition keeps its words, so has-task says 'before it goes to review' and criteria-ticked at warn says 'goes to review with unticked acceptance criteria' wherever they are listed. Today's words are what criterion 3 holds; texts per transition can come with a story that asks for them.
- children-closed and parent-open read a state's kind (accepted, cancelled) through the workflow rather than the names done and cancelled, as ADR-0301 §4 words them.
