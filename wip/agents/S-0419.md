## Decisions

- **Plan reviewed (2026-10-30).** T-1754 stands first: it waits on nothing, and no answer on TH-0760 changes it. T-1753, T-1756, T-1757, T-1759, and T-1761 wait on TH-0760. Two of their premises do not hold in the code:
  - A role file's body is the definition prompt, the same text as `.claude/agents/<name>.md` (D-0411). `planPrompt` and the other two build the start prompt, which is a different text.
  - S-0418 shipped no `continuous` trigger. Under ADR-0209 the orchestrator runs for reasons, and its triggers already come from its role file.
- **T-1754: S-0416 had already embedded the baseline.** This task now holds the embedded copies to the template's files whole, not only their front matter. The orchestrator's body had drifted and was copied over. `roles.Builtin` resolves a built-in role to the project's file, else the baseline, and leaves `roles.Load` reading only the project's files.
- **`roles.baseline` is advisory.** flai runs the baseline, and only the operator's `flai upgrade` clears the warning, as with `board.wip-limit` (`advise`). A full warning would fail `flai check --strict` in CI for every project on an older template the day it upgrades flai.
- **The warning covers all four baseline roles, the curator too.** flai carries a baseline for each of them, and the guard already runs a missing one from it.
- **Test fixtures that model a current project now carry the template's role files:** `flai/internal/metrics/testdata/good`, plus `activityRepo` and two advisory-count tests in `flai/internal/check` through `writeTemplateRoles`. The alternative was to filter `roles.baseline` out of every assertion.
- **Overlap replies.** MS-0235 (S-0523) and MS-0237 (S-0524): the hold stands until TH-0760 is answered. I said I would return to narrow or share the paths then.
- **TH-0760 (2026-10-30).** The orchestrator recommended A from ADR-0207 § 4: the body is the definition prompt, and the start prompts stay in Go. The operator asked whether A blocks a later move to data-only roles. I answered no: the start-prompt section and the triggers can follow in a story of their own. I built the part every option shares, and the operator has not yet chosen.
- **T-1753 rewritten.** `harness.Request.Builtin`, and `subAgents` makes the built-in role's definition prompt its role file's body, via `roleBody`. Description, tools, model, the `flai upgrade` refusal, and the start prompts are unchanged, and nothing is written under `.claude/` (D-0351).
- **T-1756 rewritten.** `startOf`, the start every run goes through, a defined role's included (`role.go`), now gives each built-in role its file (`builtinFile`, `roles.Builtin`). That is the shared start path, so the launchers keep their records, commands, and when.
- **T-1757 and T-1759 cancelled.** `startOf` already covers the analyzer's and the orchestrator's starts, stops, and holds. No continuous launcher exists to build on.
- **Touches narrowed.** These are off S-0419's claim: `serve/plan.go`, `replan.go`, `analyze.go`, `analysis_schedule.go`, `orchestrate.go`, `orchestrate_hold.go`, `role_continuous.go`, `serve.go`, and the six role files.
- **Paths shared with S-0523 (MS-0235) and S-0524 (MS-0237).** These are the harness files and the strategic-agents design and convention, split by section. Both agents agreed.
- **`flai check --strict` passes over `roles.baseline`.** Its help, its summary line, and the guide's list of such warnings say so. The summary test gives its project the role files.
- **The operator confirmed A on TH-0760 (14:05Z).** I reworded criteria 1 and 2 as the orchestrator's recommendation words them, with `flai edit --body-stdin`, and recorded why in the story's Notes. Both are verified and ticked.
- **Superseded: criteria 1 and 2 were not ticked.** As written, they say the start prompts are read from the role bodies, and that the start goes "through the defined-role path". The orchestrator's recommended rewording on TH-0760 waits for the operator. The code meets that rewording.
