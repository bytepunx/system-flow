## Decisions
- Operator answers, 2026-09-15: (1) no commits at task boundaries, only at story review and after acceptance; git.md says so. (2) `bytepunx/kluster` is public and stays in the baseline. (3) Three test tiers, behavior, integration, smoke, each with its own command and folder, each run after the cheaper one passes; code-quality.md and tooling.md updated, `make test`, `make integration`, `make smoke` added here and stubbed in the template, expensive Go tests skip under `-short`. (4) Operator runbooks are a new story, S-028 under E-004, not an extension of S-017 and S-019; I-005 points at it. (5) Stories awaiting acceptance are listed in the report. (6) The issues summary shows both average and total cost, most expensive first; schema and S-027 updated.
- Issue timestamps derived from commit times must be converted to UTC; the first pass labelled local time as Z and was corrected.
- Commit timing: "when a story lands" means when it moves to review and again after acceptance and archive; the git convention now says so. This replaces "commit only when asked" in both CLAUDE.md files.
- Conventional commit types fixed to feat, fix, refactor, perf, docs, test, build, ci, chore; subject `<type>: [S-nnn] ...`.
- "Stories without an epic" in the tag rule was reworded: every story has an epic (schema and check require it); a story valuable on its own may get a patch tag, an epic a minor or major.
- The I/O-abstraction rule and the no-speculative-abstraction rule were reconciled: abstractions are required at I/O boundaries and nowhere else speculatively.
- design/issues is a fifth design/ documentation type (ADR-0014) with I-nnn files, classes defect/blocker/efficiency/impression, count, cost, first/last reported, and a summary table.
- scripts/ is a standard top-level folder; the Makefile delegates to it; scripts are POSIX sh because the host shell is zsh.
- US spelling throughout the conventions, matching the operator's edits.
- Issue timestamps are taken from commit times of the sessions where they occurred, not invented.
- GoReleaser pinned to v2.18.1 in install-tools; it needs Go 1.27, which the toolchain fetches on demand while flai/go.mod stays at 1.26.
