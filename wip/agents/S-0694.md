## Decisions

- Kept the planner's tasks as drafted: they follow ADR-0254 §5a, which the operator settled on TH-0832 (D-0481), and design/system/kluster.md § A failed deploy holds the story.
- Layers: 1 T-3024 alone, since every later task reads the record and gate it writes; 2 T-3025, T-3028, T-3029 together, since each reads only T-3024's gate and their touches share no path; 3 T-3035, which documents layer 2 and regenerates the reference from T-3028's help text; 4 T-3038, which closes I-0268 once build and docs are done.
- The orchestrator's enqueue (queue_add) is not refused for kluster. T-3028's sub-agent had gated it; I reverted that. A story enters review before its deploy has run, so a gated enqueue would refuse the orchestrator's first try and leave nothing to retry it. Instead the queue lists the story ineligible with the reason kluster and batches it once the gate passes (T-3029), and the landing's acceptance is gated as well.
- The merge queue's batch reads the host actions through mergequeue.Deps.Actions, which flai serve's queue pass sets, and flai queue reads them from the configuration. T-3029's ComputeWith had no production caller until then. Its touches were widened to batch.go, batch_test.go, serve/mergequeue.go, and cmd/queue.go.
- TestFailingChecksAgainstTheNamespaceOpenOneThreadAndMoveNothing (checks_test.go) is removed. ADR-0254 §5a reverses its expectation, and TestFailingChecksAgainstTheNamespaceSendTheStoryBack in kluster_test.go covers the same thread.
