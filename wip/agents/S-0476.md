## Decisions

- The planner's seven tasks and five layers stand, reviewed against the story: T-2035 (ADR and design) first, since every later task follows its format; T-2038, T-2040, and T-2041 together, with no path in common; T-2045 after T-2040 and T-2041, since the page sends what the harness reads and saves what the route carries; T-2048 after T-2038 and T-2045, since the check must accept `answers` and the page must show them; T-2051 last, closing I-0164 once the fourteen render answered.
- Went on with the planner's design without the operator's confirmation of the recommendation on TH-0597: the story's goal asks for a proposal before building, the planner and the orchestrator made it, and the format is a fixture format that a later story can change. Recorded as ADR-0187, refining ADR-0172 and ADR-0164.
- The format, settled in ADR-0187: `answers` per example; `path` (with or without a query), optional `method`, `status` 200 to 599, `headers`, `body` (a string as text, anything else as JSON); `events` makes it an EventSource's answer. First match in file order, the path with its query before the path without; unmatched keeps ADR-0172's 501. A WebSocket is never answered. The log's `fetch` event gains the status, `EventSource` 200 or 501.
- Answers are not kept in the page's URL, since they can be large.
- The template's copy of `design/components/README.md` says what an example's answers are, so `template/CHANGELOG.md` (a shared path) gains a 1.0.108 entry, as S-0443 did for the folder.
- S-0477 shared `design/system/component-library.md` on MS-0171: S-0476 leaves its two paragraphs (end of § Commands, under § The dev server) alone.
