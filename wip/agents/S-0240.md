## Decisions

- The trace: the dashboard's new-item form posts to `flaiover/src/routes/api/items/+server.ts`, which runs the host action `item.new` (`flai/internal/hostapi/writes.go`), which runs `flai story new --body-stdin` → `itemnew.Create` → `workitem.Repo.Create` → `LintGuard` → `mdlint.Guard`; an edit that follows goes through `doc.save` → `docedit.Save`, which diffs `check.Run`, and `check` lints wip. No step on the dashboard's side writes without the lint. The step that let S-0231 through is `mdlint` itself: it does not implement MD007 (ADR-0061's rule list has no MD007), so `flai story new` and `flai task new` accept S-0231's original body too. Linting that body (from `4a2ccf0^`) with this tree's mdlint and the project's configuration reports nothing. The story's title and goal suppose otherwise; its goal, "find where, and close the gap", is met by adding the rule, so I go on without asking, and say so in the story's notes.
- Story touches changed to name `flai/internal/mdlint`, `design/issues`, and `design/system/flai-cli.md` beside the three it had.
- Plan, by layer:
  - Layer 1: T-0708 (mdlint's MD007). It waits for nothing.
  - Layer 2: T-0709 (tests that the dashboard's `item.new` and `doc.save` refuse S-0231's body naming MD007 and its line) and T-0710 (design line, notes, close I-0055). Both wait for T-0708: T-0709's tests fail until the rule exists, and T-0710 describes it. They share no path, so they run together; T-0710 is short, and I do it myself while T-0709's sub-agent works.
- ADR-0061 is accepted and is not edited: its consequences foresee a rule added when agents start to break one, so MD007 needs no new ADR.
