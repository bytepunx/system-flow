- Kept the planner's three tasks as drafted, one per layer. T-1991 waits for none and builds the generator, the benchmarks, and the reference replay. T-1993 waits for T-1991 because it proves the fix against that reference and benchmark. T-1995 waits for T-1993 because it measures the fixed replay on this repository and closes I-0155. - T-1991's profile, over a generated history of 500 stories and 1,931 tasks tuned to this repository's density (about 2 stories ready, 1 in progress, 1 in review at a time): `replayHolds` takes 2.7 s, 1.07 GB, and 14.3 M allocations per replay, 96 percent of `Compute`. Of 9,779 distinct event times, 8,326 build a `Holds`, and only 4,240 change anything a hold reads. Cumulative shares: `replayHolds` 73 percent, `NewHolds` 38, `Holds.Of` 27, `PathsOverlap` 21 (its `b+"/"` concatenations), `Holds.Overlaps` 18, `Holds.Claim` 13, the garbage collector 17.
- The fix T-1993 builds, chosen from that profile: judge the holds again only at an event time where a story event happened, or a task of a story in ready or in progress was created or moved, and otherwise carry the previous instant's held stories with their codes. Build each `Holds` from only the stories in ready or in progress, the stories their `after` names, and their tasks, kept up to date as the events apply, rather than over every item created so far. Make `PathsOverlap` compare without concatenating, which the board's holds gain from too. Per-story claim reuse is left out: with about three stories open at a time it saves little once the `Holds` is small.
