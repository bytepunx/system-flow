## Decisions

  - Layer 1: T-2674 (items pool, `store.go`) and T-2678 (folder lint pool, `folder.go`, `check.go`) wait for nothing and share no path, so they run together.

- The planner's assumptions stand: the pool is `GOMAXPROCS` capped at 16 with no setting; the lint key hashes the configuration's bytes and a rule-set version; kept items restore `Path` and `Archived` and recompute `readAs`; the kept file is written only into a `.flai-cache` that exists, as S-0531 decided (D-0402).
