## Decisions

- Reviewed the planner's four tasks against the story and kept them unchanged: `--require-pending` with exit 2 naming the release that covered what was pending, the publish step's `stop: [2]`, the documents, and the issue's close. Holding back a hand publish while an instance runs is left out, as the Planning note says: the race does no harm once the step can tell it apart.
- Layers: 1. T-2818 (flag, tag lookup, reproducing test). 2. T-2819 (formula, both copies, formulas README, template changelog) and T-2820 (docs, regenerated reference) together: both wait for T-2818, since the step names its flag and the reference is generated from its help text, and they share no path. 3. T-2821 closes I-0320 after T-2819 and T-2820, since its reason names what they deliver.
- The template component is versioned by its version file, not a tag, so for it the release that covered what was pending is its version and the commit that last set the version file, with that commit's author and time.
