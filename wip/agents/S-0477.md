Recorded as an ADR in T-2043.

T-2043 already did most of T-2032 and T-2034, so they carry only what T-2043 left: T-2032 the end-to-end case in `flai/cmd/guard_test.go` with flai's real command names, T-2034 the sentence in `design/system/agent-adapters.md`. Unlike the planner's assumption, serve is refused to read-only sub-agents and needs an ADR (ADR-0186), since it writes files ADR-0141 forbids them.
