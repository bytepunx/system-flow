## Decisions

- Plan. Layer 1: T-0760 (issues package) and T-0761 (workitem create, itemnew), no after and no common path. They are short and `issues` builds `workitem`, so they run one after the other rather than at once. Layer 2: T-0762 (CLI and MCP) waits for both, whose functions it calls. Layer 3: T-0763 (docs) waits for T-0762, whose help text it describes and regenerates into the reference.
- Time lost per cycle is cost × count ÷ cycles, cycles being the time from `first_reported` to the story's creation in `planning.cycle`s, at least one: the issue's observed rate, still counting a quiet stretch since it was last reported. The derivation is written in the story's Notes.
- The `## Impact` section is read as lines `- revenue_per_week: <amount>`, `- penalty_per_week: <amount>`, `- time_lost_per_cycle: <duration>`, the keys of `cost_of_delay.inputs`, so S-0224's analyzer writes to a format already read. A time lost given there wins over the derivation, being the analyzer's measured figure. A value that does not parse is left out and said so in Notes rather than refusing the story.
- The issue's Remediation section names the story by ID, as its instances do, not by a path link: a story's file moves to `wip/archive` on acceptance and a path link would die. Written through a hook `itemnew.Create` runs after the check keeps the story, so `--autocommit` commits the issue with the story; read from another story's worktree (`--story`), the issue is edited there and left for that story to commit.
