## Decisions

- The watch lives in flai host (`internal/host/watch.go`) and the docker side in `flai/cmd/dashboard_watch.go`. The host decides when, from looks it is given; cmd says how the container stands and starts it again. ADR-0062.
- The host restarts from a record `flai dashboard` writes (`serve/dashboard-container.json`), not from the configuration. A `--build` or `--tag` dashboard comes back as it was, and a floating tag pulls nothing. `flai dashboard stop` that leaves no project forgets the record.
- The host restarts after two looks in a row, 15 s apart, so a swap by `flai dashboard restart` or `upgrade` is not raced. The back-off is 30 s doubling to 5 min, reset after a minute of answers.
- The setting is negative (`dashboard.no_restart`), because the config's booleans load as false when absent, and the watch must be on by default.
- Docker's `--restart` policy was not used: it cannot be combined with `--rm`, and it never restarts a container that runs and does not answer.
- The refusal is judged after the proof, not at hello, so an unproven caller learns nothing. The refused flai therefore logs "connected" for a moment before the 4409 arrives. ADR-0063.
- The refused flai waits a fixed `HeldBackoff` of a minute, jittered, and does not double it: the holder may go at any time.
- `design/system/flai-cli.md` got a one-clause change on the `flai serve` row although S-0185 claims that file. The row is not one S-0185 changes, and the design must stay current.
