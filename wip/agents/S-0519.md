## Current state

Figures are logged below. I-0228 records the same limit cutting `wait_for_events`, `wait_for_work`, `verify`, and slow `task_done` calls over HTTP, committed a044c2c9. No code changed yet.

## Decisions

- Kept the planner's five tasks and their layers: T-2227 alone, then T-2228 (the ADR records what T-2227 found and the operator chose), then T-2229 (builds the bound the ADR settles), then T-2230 and T-2231 together (docs and the close, no path in common).

- The 60 s limit cuts every long call over HTTP, not only `permission_prompt`; recorded as I-0228 and left out of this story, as T-2227's work says.
- Recommended an HTTP bound of 45 s on TH-0726, 15 s below the measured 60 s limit.
