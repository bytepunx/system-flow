# S-0739 workitem's epic following, board view, task plan, holds, order, and promotion read a state's kind instead of its name

## Context

S-0737 gave `Repo.Flow()` (the workflow, baseline when `Repo.Workflow` is nil) and `workflow.Workflow` with `State`, `Kind`, `OfKind`, `Initial`, `Find`, `WithEffect`. ADR-0301 §2 defines the kinds and how an epic follows by kind.

## Current state

S-0739 back in review after TH-1216: rebased onto main, clean; close-out passed every step at 55d7edb; all five criteria ticked.

## Next steps

1. The operator accepts S-0739 or sends it back.
2. At acceptance, choose whether I-0376 becomes a backlog story.

## Decisions

- Layers: T-3203 alone first, since it adds the shared helpers (`flai/internal/workitem/flow.go`: the pull's source and target, the acceptance's source, the accepted, cancelled, and initial states, the ordered and limited states), `Board.Flow()`, and the shared test workflow that the others use; then T-3206, T-3208, and T-3210 together, which share no path.
- Package functions that take no Repo (PlanOf, PullSequence, EpicFollows, NewHolds, NewBoardView and the like) read the workflow from what they are given (the Board, the Holds) or get a form that takes it; where an exported signature would change for callers in other packages, the existing form keeps its signature and runs the baseline, so those callers change in their own E-0034 stories (S-0742 to S-0749) and this story stays in `workitem`.
- WIP limits stay in `wip/kanban/board.md` (ADR-0301 question 2); the defaults are by kind of a limited state (queue 5, active 2, waiting 3), which gives today's `ready: 5, in-progress: 2, review: 3` on the baseline.
- The shared test workflow renames `in-progress` to `doing` and adds `testing`, of kind `active` with a limit, between it and `review`, so the pull's target, two active states, and the epic's walk through an added state are all exercised.
- taskplan.go keeps its plan vocabulary, PlanReady, PlanWaiting, PlanInProgress, PlanDone, PlanCancelled and TaskSummary's fields: they are the task plan's own JSON words (S-0176), not workflow states, and each is now chosen by the kind of the state the task is in. Renaming them would change flai's JSON and its callers; criterion 1 is read as no comparison with a state's name.
- Callers outside workitem keep the baseline forms (EpicFollows, PlanOf, NewHolds, PlanCandidatesOf, PromotionCandidates, FinalizableLacks, Changes, CancelledWith) and switch to the -In forms in their own stories: check/check_items.go (epicLags, CancelledWith, NewHolds) under S-0741; transition.go's `to == Cancelled` and rules.go's PlanOf under S-0744; inbox, serve/replan.go, hostapi, mcpserver, cmd, itemedit, messages, storygit, verify, metrics under S-0742, S-0746, S-0747, and S-0749.
- The board view's JSON gains `lanes` (name, title, kind, wip_limit, takes_limit, ordered) beside the unchanged `columns`, `wip_limits`, `order`, and `breaches`, recorded in design/system/flai-cli.md's flai board row.
