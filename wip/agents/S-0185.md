## Decisions

- The values are recorded in `system-flow.lock.yaml` (`vars`), not `system-flow.yaml`: the lock is flai's own and is already rewritten by `flai new`, `flai upgrade`, and `--relock`, while the manifest is the template's rendering and human-edited. A refinement of ADR-0015's lock, so no new ADR.
- Upgrade resolves a variable from, in order: `--var`, the manifest's own field for the five standard variables, the lock's recorded value, the template's default. The manifest wins over the lock for the five because the operator may edit `name` and `description` there.
- `flai upgrade --var` for one of the five is refused, naming the manifest key to edit: a `--var` value would be overridden by the manifest at the next upgrade.
- `flai import` still writes no lock (unchanged); an imported project records its variables at its first `flai upgrade` or `--relock`.
