The adapter needs the host's `deny_protected` lift to write deny rules. `harness.Host` gains `DenyProtected`, and `startOf` sets it from `cfg.Lifts[name]`. - Layers:
  1. T-1682 (findings), T-1683 (protected list), T-1684 (instructions check). They share no path, and none waits.
  2. T-1685 (adapter start) and T-1686 (ADR). Both wait for T-1682's findings and share no path.

  4. T-1688 (serve on a stub). It waits for T-1687's adapter and T-1682's event types, which its fixture stream follows.
  5. T-1689 (docs and template). It waits for T-1684's check and T-1688, so that it describes what was built.
