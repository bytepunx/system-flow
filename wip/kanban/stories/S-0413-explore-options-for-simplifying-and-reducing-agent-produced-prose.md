# S-0413 Explore options for simplifying and reducing agent-produced prose

## Goal

There are linters that aim to ensure that English writing follows guidelines and rules in order to produce clearer, easier to read writing (Vale, Proselint, Write Good, Textlint, Coala).

There are also ways to get some of the more advanced open weight models or open source models that wouldn't typically run on user-space hardware through LoRA. The key here is the ability for flai to invoke a locally hosted model (when available) capable of producing clearer, simpler, shorter prose across the system.

I would like research conducted and documented on the tooling selection, training plans, etc. required to introduce an English linter such that output from agents running as part of this system got linted and then rewritten by a LoRA optimized model so that outputs are more easily consumed by the human operator.

There may be other notable finds or approaches that result in the same end goal: to simplify the text generated by agents of flai to improve readability and reduce the amount of text an operator or agent needs to read.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Natural language linters are evaluated and presented with a recommendation for one or more based on its suitability for this challenge
- [ ] An evaluation of Open Source models that could be refined and reduced to run easily on consumer hardware via LoRA or another approach for the express purpose of producing a model that could take in verbose, sprawling text, and output text that passes the chosen natural language linter (likely because the linter is used to produce the desired outputs as well as validate/score the new model's output)
- [ ] An exploration of potential alternatives that lead to the same outcome: simpler English prose across the flai system.
