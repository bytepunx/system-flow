### Planning

Proposed remedy, from I-0070's one instance: the parser in `flai/internal/mdlint/doc.go` marks every line after `>` as a quote and never parses what is inside, so no list in a blockquote is judged (`rules.go` says so beside MD007). Parse a blockquote's content, with its `>` markers taken out, as blocks of their own on the original lines, so that MD029 judges an ordered list in a quote as markdownlint-cli2 does. A fixture of TH-0101's entry, `> 3.` and `> 6.`, with the reference regenerated by `scripts/mdlint-fixtures.sh`, is the test that reproduces it.

Tasks, in two layers: T-0860, the parser change with its fixture, then T-0861, the design line and the issue's close, which describe the fix.
