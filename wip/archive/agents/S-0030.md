## Decisions
- Four log levels only, with fixed meanings; fatal is error plus exit. Static messages with data in fields, so events group.
- CLIs log to stderr and keep stdout for output; services log to stdout for the collector; no log files.
- OpenTelemetry is the telemetry contract; health, readiness, and Prometheus metrics endpoints are the minimum surface for a service; golden signals plus build_info are the minimum metric set; labels are a declared low-cardinality set.
- flai uses log/slog and no third-party logger; flaiover uses pino, the OpenTelemetry Node SDK, and prom-client, pinned when S-0032 lands.
- Drafts stay draft until the operator confirms; nothing in them binds until then, and prime still prints them.
