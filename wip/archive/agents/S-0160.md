# S-0160 A flai process starts in milliseconds whatever the host's PATH

## Context

Cause 5 of `design/system/server-performance.md`: `atotto/clipboard`, brought in by `huh` through `bubbles/textarea`, searched `PATH` at package load and cost every flai process about 145 ms on this host.

## Current state

Both tasks are done and committed on `story/S-0160`, rebased onto `main` after S-0156, S-0157, and S-0158 were accepted. flai asks its questions through `flai/internal/prompt`, and `huh` and the 24 modules it brought are gone from `go.mod`. `flai version` starts in a median 5.8 ms with the host's `PATH`, against 163 ms for `main`. Every criterion is checked. `make flai-test` passes lint and all three tiers. Its last step, `flai check --strict` on the repository, fails on three warnings that `main` already has: E-0003 and E-0010 are done and not archived, and TH-0032 is answered on the archived S-0149. They are the operator's to clear, not this story's. `scripts/lint-md.sh` finds one error, MD024 in TH-0035, which is also on `main`; the files this story changed lint clean.

## Next steps

1. Operator: review and accept S-0160.

## Decisions

- Replaced `huh` with a line-based prompt package of flai's own rather than keeping huh behind a build tag or replacing the clipboard module: huh imports `textarea` whatever fields are used, `go install` refuses `replace` directives, and six questions do not justify 25 modules. Recorded as ADR-0052, refining ADR-0006, since it changes a technology ADR-0006 chose.
- Prompts are written to stdout, as huh drew them, and read from stdin. Tests give the app a `prompter` over a string, which replaces the `confirm` hook.
- Cause 5's measurement is a `### Cause 5` subsection under `## After the stories` in `server-performance.md`, the section S-0156 added for each story's remeasurement, and not a paragraph after the causes table, where it conflicted with S-0158's (TH-0037).
