## Decisions

- Updates is the existing `/host` page: it is where the dashboard and flai are upgraded, restarted, and stopped (S-0081, S-0107). No new page.
- A group is a button, not a link: clicking or tapping it opens its row, as the first criterion asks; its pages are the links.
- Only the open group is bold: hovering Status on the board makes Status active and Workflow plain, and leaving the menu brings Workflow back. The story asks that a hovered parent become active; two active parents would say two places at once.
- A touch never opens or closes a group by hover, because a touch ends in a pointerleave that would close what it just opened; a tap opens by click.
- Pages outside the menu belong to a group for its row without a current page: `/items`, `/review`, `/new` to Workflow, `/edit` to Status. Otherwise those pages showed no row.
- Each group's row follows its button in the DOM and is drawn last (`order-last`), so Tab goes from a group straight to its pages.
- A `svelte-ignore a11y_no_noninteractive_element_interactions` on the nav, with a comment: its pointerleave and Escape only undo an opened group, and every group opens from its button.
