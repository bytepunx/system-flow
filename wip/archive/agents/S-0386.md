## Decisions

- Criterion 1 follows ADR-0152, accepted after the story was drafted: the guidance's `topics` stay the one list of what the design system reaches. `flai design init --project <name>` checks that the name is a sub-project in the manifest and writes it into the guidance's `topics`, rather than keeping a second record of governed sub-projects.
- Prime loads the guidance and the token file whole for the stories the guidance's topics reach, and briefs them above ADR-0050's threshold, as ADR-0152 decision 3 says; criterion 1's "briefs" is met by that.
- The design-system convention reaches a story only when the project has a guidance document and its topics reach the story (ADR-0152 decision 5); `flai prime --cat` leaves it out where there is no guidance document.
- Plan, kept from the planner: layer 1 is T-1561 (`flai/cmd/design.go`), T-1562 (`flai/internal/context`, widened to the files that select conventions), and T-1563 with T-1564 (the convention's two copies); layer 2 is T-1565 (docs and the generated reference), which waits for T-1561 and T-1562 because it describes their behaviour.
- T-1563 and T-1564 go to one sub-agent: the baseline text is one text in two copies, so T-1564 was moved to in-progress with T-1563 and closes after it.
- `flai file show`, which ADR-0152 names, does not exist. So the convention and the token file's brief tell agents to read the file, or to run `flai design show --json`.
- `design/system/design-system-tool.md` is left as it is. It is S-0380's research finding and says nothing in it is built. Its plan for S-0386 predates this decision on `--project`.
- The MCP `task_done` refused T-1561: the installed flai is 1.48.0, behind 1.51.2. Every task was closed with `scripts/flai.sh task done` instead, and this is recorded as I-0149.
