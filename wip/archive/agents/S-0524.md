- Build the remedy the planner proposed on TH-0695, a third kind of plan candidate, planned before and asked again. The operator asked for this capability on TH-0579 (I-0188's instance), and the proposal is its bounded form. The ADR was written `proposed` while the operator's confirmation on TH-0695 was pending, since the change widens the orchestrator's powers.
- The planner's five tasks stand as drafted. Layers: 1) T-2246 (the ADR) and T-2247 (the candidates), no shared path; 2) T-2248 waits for T-2247, both change `flai/cmd/plan.go`, and it carries T-2247's `thread` to the run; 3) T-2249 waits for T-2246 and T-2248, whose ADR and trigger form it documents; 4) T-2250 waits for T-2249, so the issue closes once the fix is built and recorded. I write T-2246 myself first, before T-2247's sub-agent starts, so that the ADR fixes the spec the sub-agent builds to; T-2250 is mine too.
- Shared paths: in `harness.go` and `harness_test.go` only `planPrompt`, a `Request` field for the thread, and their tests are S-0524's; S-0419 keeps `Builtin` and its test, and S-0523 keeps the cost of delay rules of `orchestratePrompt`. In the strategic-agents design and both convention copies S-0524 changes only the plan-candidate and planning-again text.
- T-2247 (task sub-agent): `PlanCandidatesOf(items, leave, asked map[string]PlanAsked)`, with `PlanCandidate.Thread` (`thread,omitempty`), filled by `operatorWroteSince` in `flai/cmd/plan.go`. An operator entry since a story's planner run also lifts `planHeld`'s "not changed since" hold, so a story that still lacks a plan comes back once the operator writes on it. Resolved threads count, as ADR-0222 does not limit them to open ones. I moved the `OperatorName` check out of the per-item loop, reworded the help text, and regenerated `docs/users/flai-reference.md` in the same task, adding it to T-2247's touches so `TestReferenceIsCurrent` stays green between tasks.
- T-2248 (task sub-agent): `orchestratorPlans` returns the admitted candidate's thread; `serve.PlanForOrchestrator(ctx, o, e, item, thread)` records `orchestrator; TH-nnnn`; `orchestratorAsked` reads both trigger forms as the orchestrator's, and `l.plan` takes the thread from the trigger into `harness.Request.AskedOn`, so `replan.go` is unchanged. `PlanStarted.Trigger` and `flai plan --json`'s `trigger` name what was recorded. No other trigger reader misreads the new form. `flaiover/src/lib/activity.ts:166` has a doc comment that lists only the operator's and the replanner's triggers; it is left alone as outside the claim, and it compares nothing. I reflowed one comment and regenerated `docs/users/flai-reference.md` in the task.

The changelog conflicts in trial merges with S-0419 and S-0512 (MS-0237, MS-0250): S-0524 keeps both bullets under one heading when it rebases after them. S-0523 will take S-0524's `plan_backlog_stories` row as it is and change only its cost of delay clauses (MS-0242).
