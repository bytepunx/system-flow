## Decisions

- Plan kept as the planner drafted it: layer 1 T-0878 (the ADR and design text the code follows); layer 2 T-0879 (the charge, in `flai/internal/serve`, `workitem`, `usage`, `mcpserver`, `cmd/activity.go`) and T-0880 (the stats report, in `flai/internal/metrics`, `cmd/stats.go`, docs) together: both wait for T-0878's decision on where the project total lives, and share no path.
- The project strategic total is worked out by `flai stats`, not written: the kind's activity document totals less that kind's `strategic` figures on the items with no parent (ADR-0095). Recording it in the document would need a migration for the planner's entries before S-0225 and a strict-parse bump, and could drift whenever a charge failed.
- The split is even over the epics, stories, and tasks named that exist, each once; threads, issues, and anything else take no share; tokens and seconds split in whole numbers with the remainder to the first items, so shares add up to the entry.
- The planner keeps ADR-0083's charge to its planned item; the analyzer charges nothing until S-0227, so its cost is all project total.
- T-0878 written by me rather than a sub-agent: it is the decision itself.
- T-0879's sub-agent added `workitem.ErrNotFound`, so only an ID no item has is left out of the split; an unreadable item keeps its share and its charge fails, warned of. The orchestrator's run end passes no items, so its last stretch stays in the project total, as the ADR says. `flai/cmd/activity_test.go` was outside the touches; widened.
- T-0880's sub-agent subtracts the rounded items cost, so items plus project equal the document's cost to four places. I wrote the `activity_log` row in `docs/users/flai.md` myself, which the T-0879 sub-agent could not reach.
