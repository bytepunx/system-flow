## Decisions

- T-2864 rewritten: ADR-0292 §2 makes the namespace's own kubeconfig the boundary and has the guard refuse requests, env, exec, and log for another story or the default namespace; the planner's draft, refusing `kubectl -n`/`--namespace` of another namespace, predates the ADR and is dropped. T-2864 now extends S-0660's and S-0763's rules to `flai checks run S-nnnn --job`, the tool `kluster_checks`, and `flai kluster env`, and takes `guard_tables.go` from T-2866.
- T-2865 rewritten: ADR-0292 §2 makes `env` and `exec` reads open while the namespace has a record, a failed one included, so `env` is not held to the head; it shares S-0763's `Cluster.AgentEnv` with `exec`. Its after on T-2863 is removed, since it reads none of T-2863's work.
- T-2863's touches gain `serve/kluster_requests.go` and its test, where S-0660's request handling lives, and `kluster/deploy.go` and its test, for the record's `checks_log` (ADR-0292 §6).
- Plan layers: 1) T-2863, T-2864, T-2865, no path in common; T-2865 runs in its own task worktree because `flai/cmd` compiles `serve` and `kluster`, which T-2863 changes. 2) T-2866, after T-2863 (its request kind and the pass's answer) and T-2865 (both are in package `cmd`). 3) T-2867, after all, since it documents what they built.
- `flai kluster env` keeps the line-wide written-plainly guard rule, so `eval "$(flai kluster env S-nnnn)"` is refused a story's agent; its help gives `flai kluster env S-nnnn > /tmp/s-nnnn.env && . /tmp/s-nnnn.env && <command>` or `flai kluster exec` instead. Letting `$(...)` around a plain flai line through would need the guard to parse command substitution, which S-0763's rule avoids.
- T-2865 regenerated `docs/users/flai-reference.md` itself, so that `TestReferenceIsCurrent` passes at its close; T-2866 and T-2867 regenerate it again.
