- Criterion 4's form follows ADR-0151 § 3. Its story has fixed criteria. Its touches are the token file, plus the stylesheet when the manifest names one. It is moved to ready and is not a draft.
- A story for `design show --story` and `design.get` with a story must be open (not done or cancelled) and have a worktree. The worktree is resolved as `doc show --story` and `file.get` resolve it.
- A proposal's tokens are read through `design.get` with `story`, not `file.get` with `story` as ADR-0151 § 2 names. The comparison needs the parsed tokens per theme, which `design.get` answers and `file.get` (raw text) does not. The worktree is resolved the same way.
- `design.get` and `flai design show --json` now name the manifest's `design.stylesheet` (`stylesheet`, left out when unset). That lets the request form put the stylesheet in the new story's touches, as ADR-0151 § 3 asks. It adds `flai/internal/designsystem/css.go` to the touches.
- `designsystem.ShowStory`'s refusals are a `Refused` error. `design.get` makes them invalid params and makes a worktree that cannot be read an internal failure. `Proposals` also counts a token file inside a folder git does not track yet.
- `ProposalCompare`'s headings start at `h3`, below the Design page's `Proposals` `h2`.
- The design's two extra criteria (tweak a proposal on its branch, the guidance's threads by heading) are asked on TH-0457. Until it is answered, the story's criteria stand as written.
