# S-0329 Display all time stamps across the site in local time

## Context

flaiover formats flai's UTC strings in each component, most with its own `at` helper that appends `UTC`, and the charts set `useUTC: true`. The story adds one formatter and routes every displayed time through it; flai and the API keep recording UTC.

## Current state

All five tasks are done and both criteria are ticked. The close-out passed every step: check, vitest, markdown, and flaiover prettier and eslint. Every time the dashboard shows goes through `flaiover/src/lib/localtime.ts`.

## Next steps

1. Operator review and acceptance of S-0329.

## Decisions

- Kept the planner's five tasks as drafted: they cover every display site a search of `flaiover/src` finds, and T-1184's sweep catches any left.
- Layers: T-1180 alone, since the three view tasks call its `localtime.ts` and depend on its pinned test zone; then T-1181, T-1182, and T-1183 together, as they share no file; then T-1184, which documents what they built and sweeps after them.
- Worked on the planner's assumptions on TH-0315 (no operator answer when the story started): times as `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM <zone>`, chart buckets stay UTC as `flai stats` counts them, cron schedules stay described as UTC, relative ages and raw markdown text unchanged.
- T-1183 drew each UTC day or week bucket at the local midnight of its own date (`place` in `charts.ts`), so a bar sits on its date's tick in any zone; tooltips name the bucket by flai's date. Bucketing is untouched, as ADR-0056 and `metrics.md` define it.
- T-1184's sweep found three helpers T-1181 to T-1183 did not name: `setBy` and the forecast delivery in `planning.ts`, and `health` in `settings.ts`. They now go through `localTime`.
- flai's own messages, such as a refusal naming when a planner started, carry raw UTC times. `localTimes` in `localtime.ts` rewrites each such time in a text, and `DismissibleNotice.svelte`, the banner every action's answer is shown in, applies it. This was preferred over changing flai, which does not know the browser's zone.
- Layer 2's sub-agents could not run prettier or eslint, since `flai test` runs only vitest for flaiover (I-0105); the close-out's flaiover tier checks them.
