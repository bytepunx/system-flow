# S-0221 The orchestrator accepts stories in review when permitted

## Context

With `orchestration.permissions.accept_reviews` on, the orchestrator accepts a story in review through `flai accept --by orchestrator`, when the verifier passed at the branch head, every criterion is ticked, the diff stays within the touches, and no thread is open. ## Decisions

- The planner's plan is kept as it stands: layer 1 T-0898 (the ADR fixes the conditions and the evidence every other task implements); layer 2 T-0900, T-0903, T-0905, each after T-0898 and sharing no path; layer 3 T-0907, after T-0900 (it calls the preview option) and T-0903 (its permission check must agree with the guard); layer 4 T-0909 and T-0911, after T-0907, whose output they show and describe.
- How flai knows the verifier passed: the planner's recommendation on TH-0134, which the operator resolved without objection. The orchestrator runs its own verifier on the story's worktree and gives `flai accept` the commit it verified (`--verified`); flai refuses when that is not the branch head.
- The evidence is a markdown file given with `--evidence <file>` (`-` for standard input): a `Verdict:` line, and one list item per acceptance criterion, `- <n>: <changed files that meet it>`. flai refuses an orchestrator's acceptance whose evidence leaves a criterion without a file the branch changed: that is how "never accepts a story whose criteria it cannot check against the diff" is held by flai and not only by the prompt.
- flai writes the evidence under `### Accepted by the orchestrator` in the story's Notes, with the verified commit, in the acceptance commit, and returns it in `--json`. The orchestrator logs the acceptance with `activity_log`, as it logs every action, naming the story and the commit.
- `--by orchestrator` selects the orchestrator's flow whoever runs it; under `FLAI_ROLE=orchestrate`, any other `--by` is refused, by the guard and by flai.
- `.claude/agents/orchestrator.md` and its template copy cannot be written by the installed flai's permission prompt (TH-0169): T-0905 wrote them to the worktree's git-ignored `.flai-cache/s0221/` (a `/tmp` write is refused as well, I-0082), and the operator copied them in on TH-0170.

- Paths the workflow writes itself (`wip`, `design/issues`, an experiment's results) never count as outside the touches (T-0900): otherwise every story that records an issue would be blocked. Recorded in `strategic-agents.md`, not in the ADR, as a refinement.
- `docs/operators/settings.md` joined the story's touches: T-0907's flags regenerate its flag index.
- I added `by` to the acceptance's `--json` myself (`preview`, T-0900's path, already done) so the review page can name who accepted.
- TH-0177 (a `design/issues/summary.md` conflict with S-0292) resolved without narrowing: the file is flai's issue table, and whichever story is accepted second keeps both rows.
