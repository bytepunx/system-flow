## Decisions

- The planner's six tasks are kept. T-1769 and T-1770 no longer wait for T-1768: the story's agent fixed the formula format below before the first layer, and each task's prompt carries it, so the ADR records it while the loader and the field are built.
- Layers: 1, T-1768 (ADR), T-1769 (loader), T-1770 (front matter field), which share no path. 2, T-1771 (check) after T-1769, which it calls, and T-1772 (commands) after T-1769 and T-1770, which it builds on; the two share no path. 3, T-1773 (docs and template) after T-1771 and T-1772, since it describes the finding and the commands.
- The formula format: `design/workflows/formulas/<name>.md`, the name the file's stem; front matter `title` (the instance story's title), `updated`, `status`, `name`, `description` (the story's goal), `nature` (default `improvement`), `inputs` (each `name`, `description`, optional `default`; one without a default is required), and `steps` (each `id`, `title`, `run`, `after`). `run` is a mapping with exactly one of `flai: <arguments after flai>`, `script: <scripts/... command line>`, or `role: <name>` with `prompt:`. An input is used as `{{name}}` in a step's title or run, and in the formula's title. Body above the baseline marker is the template's description; `## Project additions` below it is the project's. Unknown keys are read past, so S-0422 can add a schedule.
- `--epic` is optional: a story need not belong to an epic (`flai/internal/workitem/create.go`).
- T-1772 also took `flai/internal/workitem/create.go`, since `NewOptions` carries the field to creation, and `flai/internal/guard/guard.go`: every sub-agent may run `flai formula list` and `show`, and `flai formula run` is listed with the commands that author an item.
- `flai formula run` rolls back what it made when an item is refused, and commits once under `--autocommit`, so a run is all or nothing (T-1772's sub-agent's choice, kept).
- `template/template.yaml` is not bumped, though T-1773's Work asked for it: `flai release` bumps it at publish (the last five bumps are publish commits), and the changelog line goes under `## Unreleased` (git.md).
- `CLAUDE.md`'s layout row for `design/workflows/` names `formulas/` too.
- Overlap with S-0553 (in review) in the three `strategic-agents.md` files: the `updated` lines and an adjacent bullet. S-0421 resolves them at its rebase if accepted second; agreed on MS-0283, settled.
