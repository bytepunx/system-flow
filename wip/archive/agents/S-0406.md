## Decisions

- Kept the planner's eight tasks, with three changes to the plan:
  - T-1685 and T-1686 now wait for T-1682. The adapter's flags and the ADR on where the configuration lives rest on what the pinned release's source says: its CLI flags, how it loads `OPENCODE_CONFIG` or inline configuration, and how it merges a project's `opencode.json`.
  - T-1688 also touches `flai/internal/serve/agents.go`. The adapter needs the host's `deny_protected` lift to write deny rules. `harness.Host` gains `DenyProtected`, and `startOf` sets it from `cfg.Lifts[name]`. That is one line in serve; the refusals of ADR-0131 (`startAllowed`) already read the capabilities and stay as they are.
- Layers:
  1. T-1682 (findings), T-1683 (protected list), T-1684 (instructions check). They share no path, and none waits.
  2. T-1685 (adapter start) and T-1686 (ADR). Both wait for T-1682's findings and share no path.
  3. T-1687 (configuration and deny rules). It waits for T-1683's list, T-1685's adapter, and T-1686's decision, and touches `opencode.go` after T-1685.
  4. T-1688 (serve on a stub). It waits for T-1687's adapter and T-1682's event types, which its fixture stream follows.
  5. T-1689 (docs and template). It waits for T-1684's check and T-1688, so that it describes what was built.
- Pinned OpenCode v1.19.5, whose repository has moved to `anomalyco/opencode` (T-1682). Its findings are read from source, not run.
- ADR-0260: the configuration reaches OpenCode through `OPENCODE_CONFIG_CONTENT` (flai's MCP entry) and `OPENCODE_PERMISSION` (the deny rules), with no file written. `OPENCODE_CONFIG` was rejected because it loads before the project's own files, which would override flai's entry. A git-ignored `opencode.json` was rejected because it clashes with a project's committed one.
- The opencode list in `protected` is built from Claude Code's, plus `.opencode`, `opencode.json`, `opencode.jsonc`, and `AGENTS.md`. `protected.Path` answers for every list, so these paths are now protected on claude-code too. That makes S-0406 itself operator-accepted (ADR-0106), since it adds `template/root/opencode.json`.
- `instructions.missing` sits on `CLAUDE.md`, so a scoped check passes over it unless the story changes that file. The metrics good fixture gained a `CLAUDE.md`.
- The container runs no opencode inner harness: `harness.ContainerInners()` leaves opencode out, and the adapter refuses a container start.
- `effort` becomes `--variant`. OpenCode names its variants by reasoning effort (`provider/transform.ts`). Without this, every story here would be refused, because the project default `effort: high` is inherited and a story cannot unset it.
- Resume: flai serve's `endRun` records the `ses_` session an adapter implementing `harness.SessionReader` reads from the log. The adapter refuses to resume any other session.
- Roles under `--guard none` are not refused by the adapter. OpenCode's own sub-agents run unguarded, and the roles' models are not applied until S-0409. The operator docs say so.
- Known limits of `--deny-protected` on OpenCode, documented for S-0407's guard plugin to close: writes through `bash` are not covered; a project's `agent.<name>.permission.edit` rules beat flai's; a project's own `edit` object naming a protected pattern before `"*": "allow"` keeps its earlier position.
- S-0587 overlaps on `harness.go`, `serve/agents.go`, `serve_actions.go`, and `settings.md` (MS-0346). Agreed: whichever story lands second keeps both sides. The trial merge conflicts on `docs/operators/settings.md` only.
