## Decisions

- The project default is `prime.budget` in `system-flow.yaml`, not the host's `config.json`: the criterion says a project sets it, and the host configuration is one for every project on the machine, while the MCP server serves several projects.
- The budget counts the header and the body of the text pack, so what `flai prime --story` prints is what fits.
- Briefs print grouped: design briefs under `briefs` and ADR briefs as one line each under `decisions`, with the fetch instruction once per group and a brief's first reason plus a count of the rest; the JSON keeps every reason. The ADR briefs take one header row between them. This took the S-0138 briefs from 28 KB to 23 KB.
- `Step` also briefs an ADR that refines a named or briefed one (`refines ADR-nnnn`), as ADR-0049 §The pack step 3 says.
- The ranked step tries every candidate in rank order, not five and five, and keeps what fits; an ADR whole first, then its best section.
- A decision sentence under five words (a bold lead-in such as "MCP over HTTP.") takes the next sentence with it.
- TH-0032 answered C (2026-09-29T03:51:59Z): keep 80 KB and every brief, provided the agent reads a full document when a brief bears on the story.
- Briefs are kept when they take the pack over budget (`exceeded: briefs`, nothing ranked): the designer's choice C on TH-0032. The S-0138 criterion was reworded from "measures under 80 KB" to "is measured and the measurement is in the notes" on that answer.
- E-0001 and E-0004 archived with `flai archive` in the main checkout: the operator moved them to done without archiving, which failed the smoke tier's `flai check --strict`; archiving an accepted item is the convention's follow-up without asking.
- `flai check`'s `adr.decision` fixture ADRs in `cmd/adr_test.go` got a full stop, since "d" is not a sentence.
