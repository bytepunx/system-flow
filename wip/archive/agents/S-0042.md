# S-0042 Agent presence, activity, and the designer's inbox

## Context
The designer should see which agents are active and what they are doing, and have one inbox of things that need a human. Everything is derived from files: narratives under `wip/agents` (front matter `stream`, `title`, `updated`, `agent`, `session`; a `## Log` of `### <timestamp>` entries; `## Open questions`), threads under `wip/threads`, item front matter (status, blocked intervals, touches). The dashboard's server already reads items and threads (`src/lib/server/repo.ts`), emits change events from a watcher over SSE (`sse.ts`), and names the designer as the manifest's owner (`threads.ts`). `wip.overlap` is a rule in `flai check` (Go).
