Reason: keeps component tags meaning one thing while still using them through the manifest; stated on the thread.
- TH-0021 answered 1b 2a 3 (every convention `[all]` for now) 4b 5b; `topics` replaces `applies_to`.
- With no budget, ranking adds a fixed five ADRs and five design sections. Reason: the replay's top 5 recovered most of what links miss, and a count bounds the pack without a size budget the designer declined.
