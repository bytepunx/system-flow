## Decisions

- The queue lands a story by running `flai accept <story> --by <enqueuer> --batch <id>` as a subprocess of flai serve. The acceptance steps are unexported in `package cmd`, which serve cannot import. A subprocess keeps one acceptance flow, so a landing matches an acceptance by hand.
- `--batch` makes the preview take the batch's verification in place of the story head's: the batch must be named, not dropped, and passed or bisected; the story must be next to land with its branch at the head the batch rebased; and main must be where the landing left it. The accept's own sync then rebases the story onto main as the batch did; the acceptance commits between landings change only `wip/`.
- Landing and bisecting need each batched story's verified and rebased heads, and the bisection and landing must be kept in the queue's state. So `Batch` gets `Heads`, `Bisect`, and `Landing`, with their types in a new `progress.go` (T-1909). The state.go and batch.go lines are asked of S-0401 on MS-0143.
- Bisection checks each prefix out at its last story's rebased head in the batch's worktree, rather than rebuilding it.
- The orchestrator's landing needs no `--evidence`. S-0403 makes the orchestrator enqueue with evidence; until then the queue's eligibility (verify, criteria, threads) stands in for it, and the notes name the batch's verified commit.
- Plan, by `after` and touches:
  - Layer 1: T-1642 (the ADR) and T-1909 (heads, bisect, and landing types). Neither waits.
  - Layer 2: T-1644 (bisect and send-back), after T-1909's types; T-1660 (the dashboard panel), after T-1909's state shape.
  - Layer 3: T-1659 (land and advance), after T-1644, whose `Bisect` and `SendBack` `Advance` calls.
  - Layer 4: T-1643 (`flai accept --batch`), after T-1659's `Landable` and `Landed`.
  - Layer 5: T-1669 (the serve driver), after T-1643 and T-1659.
  - Layer 6: T-1677 (docs), after everything it describes.
- T-1660 lost its `after` on T-1909: the panel reads only the JSON shape `progress.go` fixed, which was written before T-1909 closed, so it ran beside it.
- MS-0143 got no answer while S-0401 finished its code tasks, so S-0400 added the agreed lines and said so there. The `batch.go` line meets S-0401's `rb.out == nil` change at whichever story syncs second.
- The queue's send-back starts the story's agent again, as the forge's does: `startSentBack` in `serve/agents.go` now also reads the queue's `OutcomeFailing` outcomes. Without this, the agent would never hear of the thread. The harness's send-back prompt gets a `Queue` wording, since the queue pushes nothing. The queue resolves its send-back thread once the story is in review again at a new commit (`ResolveSentBack`); otherwise the open thread would keep the story ineligible for good. T-1669's touches widened for these.
- Criterion 1's evidence: the queue keeps no evidence on an enqueue yet, which S-0403 adds. The orchestrator's landing writes `### Accepted by the orchestrator` with the batch's verified commit and `- Batch: <id>`, and the evidence when one is given.
- Criterion 5: main moving is found when the batch's verification ends and before each landing, not in the middle of a tier run. The batch is then dropped and built again from the new main.
