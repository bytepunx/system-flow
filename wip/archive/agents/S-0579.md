## Decisions

- The plan is four layers, one task each: T-2481 (deploys), then T-2482 (teardown and promotion), then T-2483 (checks), then T-2484 (documents). T-2482 extends T-2481's pass in `flai/internal/serve/kluster.go`. T-2483 starts the checks from that pass and reads its records, so it waits for T-2482, which shares the file. T-2484 documents what the three built. The planner had T-2482 and T-2483 in one layer. That no longer works once T-2483 touches `kluster.go`.
- T-2483's work is rewritten. flai serve starts no checks run on its own today. A run is started by `flai checks run` or the dashboard (`flai/cmd/checks.go`). So `RunChecks` gains an environment. A run for a story whose namespace passed at its head gets ADR-0254 §4's variables. With `checks` on, the pass starts a run after a passing deploy. `flai/cmd/checks.go` is added to the touches.
- Criterion 4 says a failed deploy "moves nothing". ADR-0254 §5a says it sends the story back to in-progress. It also gives `flai accept` a `kluster` blocker that no criterion names. The story is built to its criteria: the thread is opened, the story stays in review, and there is no blocker. `design/system/kluster.md` says what is built and what §5a leaves to a later story. The gap is recorded as I-0268, which the review page lists, so that the operator chooses at acceptance whether it becomes a story. It is not an open question: the move to review refuses one, and the answer does not change this story.
- A failed promotion opens its thread on `system-flow.yaml`, not on an epic, as ADR-0254 §5a says.
- What the task sub-agents (forks) did:
  - T-2481 kept a "tried" file per namespace under `.flai-cache/kluster/serve/`, so a failed head is not retried every minute. It reused `kluster.Cluster.Up` for the bring-up, writing to the dashboard's `up.log`.
  - T-2482 found acceptances through the curate scheduler's `news` and `accepted`, from the pass's start. It opens a failed teardown's thread on `system-flow.yaml` once the story is archived, to avoid a `threads.archived` finding.
  - T-2483 added `RunChecksWith` (with a context and an environment) rather than a `ChecksConfig.Env` field, since `checks_config.go` was outside its touches. Its sub-agent ran `go test` by hand once, against the brief. The `flai test` runs that followed passed.
  - No sub-agent reported interference.
- `docs/operators/index.md` keeps its heading `The kluster host action (S-0577)`: renaming it would break links in `docs/operators/settings.md` and `docs/users/flai.md`.
- S-0581 was accepted while this story ran. Its MS-0331 notice about `design/system/kluster.md` and `docs/users/flaiover.md` closed before a reply could be sent. Its edits are in other sections, and the sync after T-2483 rebased cleanly onto it.
