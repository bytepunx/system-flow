## Decisions

- The board's JSON shape is fixed before the code, so that the flai and the dashboard tasks run together. `held` gains `asked`, a list of `{by, conversation, at}`: the holding story, the conversation flai serve asked its agent in, and when. A card gains `shared`, the shares in force where its story is the held side, each `{holder, held, paths, split, by, at, conversation}`. `Share` gains `conversation`, filled from the conversation's `id`, and never written to its front matter.
- The ask is read in `workitem`, as S-0334's shares are, from the open conversations under `wip/messages`. An ask is an entry by `flai` for the held story, since it last entered ready, in a conversation that names the holding story. No caller changes: `Repo.Holds` reads them, so `flai board`, `board.get`, MCP `board`, and flai serve's activity all carry them.
- The dashboard's held card reads `asked` from the activity's `hold`, which flai serve builds with `Repo.Holds`. The shared card reads `shared` from the board card, so T-1326 also touches `flaiover/src/lib/server/board.ts` and the board page.
- Layers: (1) T-1324, T-1325, and T-1326 together, since they share no path and the shape is fixed. T-1326 loses the planner's `after` on T-1324 for that reason. (2) T-1327, the docs, after all three, since it describes what they built.
- The card's asked and shared lines link their conversation, as criteria 2 and 3 say. The card is one draggable `<a>`, and a link cannot hold another. So the lines sit in a row just under that link, drawn as part of the card, as the board page already puts the card's controls beside the link and never in it. Each links `/messages#MS-nnnn`. `Messages.svelte` gives each conversation its ID as an anchor and scrolls to it on the first load. The T-1326 sub-agent had named the conversation in plain text; I changed it in review.
- `workitem` keeps its own copies of the entry heading's format and of `readySince`, since `messages` imports `workitem`. Moving them would widen the story into `messages`.
- Sub-agents: T-1324 (Go fields and tests), T-1325 (shares in `Messages.svelte`), T-1326 (card, helpers, board mapping), and T-1327 (docs). None reported interference. The installed flai 1.39.13's `task_done` committed all three layer-1 tasks' files under T-1324 (9f67200a) and widened its touches to them; T-1324 was done and refused the edit back.
