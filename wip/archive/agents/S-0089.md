# S-0089 Inbox should not continue showing open items for stories that moved to review

## Context

`inbox.designer` (flai's own composition of what needs the designer) added a "question" entry for every hand-written bullet under a narrative's `## Open questions`, unconditionally: a thread mirrored there already tracks answered/resolved on its own, but a freeform bullet has no such tracking, so it lingered forever, and nothing stopped a story moving to review while one was still open.

## Current state

Done. Two independent fixes, both in `flai/`: `flai move` (and the MCP and dashboard paths behind it, since all three share `workitem.Repo.Transition`) now refuses a story's move to review while its narrative still has an open question, naming it (`internal/workitem/rules.go`); `inbox.designer` separately leaves out question entries once a story no longer needs the designer — review, done, cancelled, or archived (`internal/hostapi/people.go`) — independent of the move rule, for anything already in that state before this shipped or moved there by hand. `OpenQuestions` moved from `hostapi` to the shared `workitem` package (one implementation, not two).
