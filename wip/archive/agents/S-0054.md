# S-0054 Board cards: larger detail text under a thin divider

## Context
The bottom row of a board card (nature, type, blocked flag, parent ID) is set at 10 pixels and sits directly under the title. The operator wants it larger and separated from the title by a thin line. The card is `flaiover/src/lib/components/BoardCard.svelte` (S-0048); its test is beside it. S-0055 (colour coding) changes the same component and is ready behind this story. Work is in `.flai-cache/worktrees/S-0054` on `story/S-0054`.

## Current state
Done and committed on `story/S-0054` as 88b808f, ready for review. The detail row is `text-xs` (12 px, the title's size, the operator's choice) set once on the row, under a `border-t border-line` divider with 8 px above and below, and it wraps (`flex-wrap`, parent `shrink-0 ml-auto`). The top row is unchanged, as the operator chose. Two new component tests cover the divider, the single size, and the wrap; both fail against main's card. `docs/users/flaiover.md` and `design/system/flaiover-dashboard.md` describe the row. `make flaiover-test` (lint, svelte-check, 176 tests) and `make flaiover-build` pass.

Browser check on the branch's dev server against this repository's real board, epics and tasks shown, 26 cards, in both themes: at 1280 and 1872 wide (six columns, cards 159 px) every row computes to 12 px, the divider is 1 px in the theme's line colour, no card or page overflows, and the parent's right edge is the row's; four rows wrap, the ones that carry three items ("improvement task S-0054", "research BLOCKED E-0006"), with the parent alone on the second line at the right. At 820 and 390 wide (one column) nothing wraps.
