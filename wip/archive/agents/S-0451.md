- Fix: `itemnew.Create` keeps a new `wip.overlap` as a warning, as `itemedit` does. The claim watch already tells both stories and messages the other. Proposed from the instance on MS-0148 and built.

Between two stories in progress, an overlap is advisory and holds nothing, so a share would have nothing to lift once creation stops refusing. The agreed split stands in the conversation, as MS-0148 settled.
