# S-0055 Board cards are colour coded by type and nature in pastels

## Context
The operator wants a card's kind recognisable at a glance from colour: its type (epic, story, task) and its nature (what they called QoS), in pastels, with the text kept; colour blindness checks on the palette are waived because the text says the same thing. The card is `flaiover/src/lib/components/BoardCard.svelte`, now with S-0054's divider; colours are tokens in `flaiover/src/routes/layout.css` with contrast checked in `src/lib/theme.test.ts` (S-0044). Work is in `.flai-cache/worktrees/S-0055` on `story/S-0055`.

## Current state
The board's coding is committed on `story/S-0055` as 034f87b, and the extension the operator asked for at review as 1048d87; ready for review again. Option A, the operator's choice: `BoardCard.svelte` takes its background from the nature (`bg-nature-*`) and a 4 px left stripe from the type (`border-l-type-*`), through the maps in `$lib/cardcolour.ts`; an unknown nature or type keeps the plain card; hovering strengthens the other three edges and leaves the stripe. A blocked card has a 1 px danger ring, the dragged card is faded and dashed. `BoardLegend.svelte` above the columns is built from the same maps. Ten nature tokens and six type tokens are in `layout.css` (both themes) and exposed to Tailwind; the pastels are the softened set. `theme.test.ts` holds ink, muted, and danger at 4.5:1 on every tint (lowest measured 5.45). Tests: one case per nature and type, the fallback, blocked, dragging, the legend, and that every class in the maps is a token in both themes; 11 of the card's tests fail against main's card. `make flaiover-test` (lint, svelte-check, 224 tests) and `make flaiover-build` pass. The design document and the user docs describe the coding, the legend, and the waiver with its reason.

Browser check on the branch's dev server against this repository's real board, epics and tasks shown, 25 cards (16 feature, 5 research, 2 improvement, 2 remediation; 4 epics, 14 stories, 7 tasks), in both themes at 1280 wide and at 390: every card's computed background equals its nature token and its left border is 4 px in its type token; every legend entry matches its token; no card, legend, or page overflows. The blocked S-0052 computes a 1 px ring in the danger colour and was seen on its lilac tint in the dark theme; a dragstart on S-0057 gave opacity 0.5 with a dashed border on its tint, cleared by dragend. No `experiment` item exists, so that tint was seen only in the legend and in the options mock-up.

Extension at review (T-0209), committed as 1048d87: `KindChips.svelte` writes the type beside a swatch of its stripe colour and the nature on its tint, from the same maps. The item page's header uses it; an item hit in search is tinted and striped (`stripeClass`), the hit now carrying `nature`, and a document hit stays plain; the item table under the cycle time charts shows the nature chip. Chart series keep `NATURE_SLOT`'s saturated colours; `cardcolour.test.ts` holds each within 25 degrees of hue of its tint in both themes. `make flaiover-test` (232 tests) and the build pass. Browser check on the real repository in both themes: `/items/S-0061` header chips equal their tokens; searching "inbox" gave 30 hits, 16 items all tinted and striped to their tokens and 14 documents all plain; `/charts/cycle-time` showed 47 nature chips equal to their tokens, none overlapping after the rows were given room. The chart canvas stayed light in the dark screenshot because the check set `data-theme` by hand, which the chart's theme state does not watch; the theme button was not used.
