The built-in roles' launchers (replanner, analysis and curate schedulers) read their files' triggers and judge them with the defined roles' functions, keeping their own queues, records, and journal entries, so that `replan_test.go`, `analysis_schedule_test.go`, and `curate_schedule_test.go` pass unchanged; moving their starts into `role_events.go` whole was rejected for that reason.
- MS-0326: in `docs/operators/index.md` S-0554 changes only what it says of the strategic roles' start prompts, triggers, and schedules, and leaves S-0510's "In a container" bullet alone.
- S-0566 changes one sentence of `orchestratePrompt` (uncommitted files under wip, design, and the shared paths are committed with the acceptance, ADR-0248). Whichever lands second carries it: if S-0566 lands first, the orchestrator's `start` in the three role files takes its sentence at the rebase. S-0566 was accepted first: at T-2359's sync the `harness.go` conflict was resolved by keeping S-0554's side, and the sentence was carried into the three `orchestrator.md` start fields in that rebase step (MS-0328).
- T-2357 ran in its own worktree (`task/T-2357`), since it changed `harness`, which T-2358's serve tests build; its files were copied back and the worktree removed.
- T-2359's sub-agent wrote schedule helpers (`startCadences`, `followSchedules`, `markAll`, `servedPlainly`) in `analysis_schedule.go` beside a copy in the replanner. I moved them to `role_schedule.go` and made the replanner use them, so the three built-in launchers follow schedules with one code path.
- I-0262: a defined role that takes items with `{schedule: ..., when: each-ready-story}` now passes `flai check`, but `lookAt` still skips it, as it did before. Recorded rather than built: it needs per-item held-start bookkeeping in `role_schedule.go`, beyond this story's criteria.

- MS-0326: S-0510 and S-0554 both extend the `flai check` row of `design/system/flai-cli.md`; whichever lands second keeps both sides.
