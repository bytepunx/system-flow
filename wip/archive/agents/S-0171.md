# S-0171 Add a "New {item}" button to the top of the item pages

## Context

The operator wants to make another item of the type they are viewing from its page, without going back to the board. The dashboard makes epics and stories through `/new` (`NewItemForm.svelte`), which already takes `?type=` and `?status=`.

## Decisions

- No link on a task's page: the dashboard creates only epics and stories, and tasks are the agent's to write (design, `/items/:id`). Recorded in the story's notes and the design.
- A new story made from a story's page starts under that story's epic, through `?parent=` on `/new`; an epic closed since is dropped, because the form offers open epics only. A refinement of the `/new` view, recorded in the design.
- The link shows whatever the viewed item's state, when the dashboard can write, as the board's "+ new" does.
- `svelte/no-navigation-without-resolve` is disabled around the link, whose path is `resolve('/new')` with a query added, as the board and the layout do.
