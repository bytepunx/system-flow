# S-0204 The new-item and edit forms take cost of delay inputs in a collapsed panel

## Context

S-0199 gave epics and stories `cost_of_delay` (inputs, value, one `by`/`at`), hostapi `item.edit` already takes it, and S-0203 gave `workitem.Create` a `CostOfDelay`. The dashboard forms take none of it, `flai story/epic new` has no flags for it, and the item page shows it as plain lines with no stale note. Main carried conflict markers in `design/system/work-hierarchy.md` when this story opened (I-0066).

This takes the story into `flai/cmd`; touches widened.

  - Layer 3: T-0770 (item page inputs, value stamp, stale note; `planning.ts`, items route). After T-0769, which settles where the stamps live.
  - Layer 4: T-0771 (dashboard design and user guide). After T-0768 and T-0770, whose behaviour it describes.
- 2026-10-03: S-0206 (in progress) also touches `flai/internal/workitem`; T-0769 keeps its change to `planning.go` and the stamping in `itemedit`/`create.go`, and stream sync's trial merge will say if they meet.
