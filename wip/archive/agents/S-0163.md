# S-0163 Improved Charts

## Context

S-0163 replaces the usage charts of S-0143 with charts of what is spent over time: tokens per bucket of time, per item of each type, per agent minute, and per dollar, and cost per bucket and per item, by model. Worktree `.flai-cache/worktrees/S-0163`, branch `story/S-0163`.

What the data is (read with `flai stats --json` on 2026-09-29): 13 stories and 36 tasks done carry usage, no epic does, every one was completed on 2026-09-29, and one model (`claude-opus-5-5`) did all of it. An item's usage is a total with agent seconds: nothing in it says when within the item the tokens were spent.

## Decisions

- Spend is laid out over time by the moment an item entered done, in buckets. An item's usage is one total, so its completion is the only moment it can be placed at.
- The bucket is an hour, a day, or a week, a day by default. The criteria ask for tokens per day; every item with usage here was completed on one day, so a chart by day alone would show one point today.
- "Average tokens spent per day over time" is drawn as the tokens of each bucket with the running mean per bucket beside them. The goal also asks for the rate in minutes, so the token rate chart stays and becomes tokens per agent minute over time.
- "Average per work item type" is the mean per item done in the bucket, one series per type. The report therefore carries the series for epics, stories, and tasks at once, whatever `--type` is. Each type is summed on its own, so a story's tokens are never added to its tasks'.
- "For each model" and "grouped by the model used" are a grouping of the charts per item: by type, or by model for the selected type.
- The cost, completion over time, and completion against cost charts of S-0143 stay as they are. The story asks for better charts and names only the token rate as wrong. Removing a chart is the operator's to ask for.
- `tokens_per_hour` stays in `flai stats --json` beside the new `tokens_per_minute`. It was released in flai 1.25, and an older dashboard reads it.
- Each task is committed on its own, as the operator's start instruction says. The git convention says to commit when the story lands. The operator's word takes precedence.
- Models and item types have a mark each on the line charts, as well as a colour. The opus and fable colours of S-0143's palette (slots 0 and 6) are close for a reader with deuteranopia: the dataviz validator's all-pairs check gives 0.6 in light and 2.2 in dark, where 8 is the target. The palette's slots are not changed here. The stacked bars and the completion charts still tell the two apart by colour and legend alone; moving fable to another slot is proposed in the story's notes as a follow-up.
- The metrics change gets an ADR, written as accepted: the designer's acceptance of the story accepts it, as with ADR-0051.
