## Decisions

- Added T-1895: the kept state had no count of the approvals the base branch requires, and ADR-0165 left that to this story. It extends the forge contract with `RequiredApprovals` and keeps `required_approvals` in the status.
- Plan, in layers:
  - First: T-1895 (forge contract and kept state) and T-1624 (the ADR). They share no path and wait for nothing.
  - Second: T-1623 (preview blockers). It waits for T-1895 because it reads `required_approvals` and `Status.Decisions`.
  - Third: T-1625 (refusals and tests across the three acceptances) and T-1626 (dashboard confirmation). Both wait for T-1623's blockers and its `--json` shape, and they share no path.
  - Fourth: T-1627 (design and docs). It waits for T-1624, T-1625, and T-1626 so that it describes what they built.
- I am treating TH-0463 question 3 as answered "the orchestrator never approves": the operator finalized S-0395 with that criterion. It is also how flai already works, since no code approves a review. I asked for confirmation on a thread on S-0395.
- T-1626's touch moved from `routes/review/[id]/+page.svelte` to `lib/components/Review.svelte` and its test. The page only renders that component, which holds the confirmation.
- T-1625 needed no change to `flai/cmd/accept.go` or `hostapi/writes.go`. Both refuse on any blocker, and the dashboard's `accept.run` runs the same `flai accept`. I took the three files out of S-0395's touches.
- The orchestrator's convention, the template's baseline and this project's copy, names the forge blockers in its `accept_reviews` steps. Its never line now includes approving a review. I folded the rule into that line to stay under the 120-line limit.
- The forge blockers are their own `forge_blockers` list in the preview, with code, name, and URL. They are not added to `orchestrator_blockers`, because they apply to every acceptance.
- The installed flai 1.57.0 is behind 1.60.0 in the history, so the MCP `task_done` refused to close tasks (ADR-0142). I closed every task with the tree's `scripts/flai.sh task done`.
