## Decisions

- One step is followed from every selected section, those chosen by topics included. ADR-0047 says "the ADRs a selected section links". The replay shows this is what reaches the ADRs: 91 of 93 without the story's own links, against 68 without the step. TH-0029 raises the size it costs.
- Links count as markdown links (a `#fragment` naming a heading narrows the link to that section and the ones below it), a document's repository path written out in the text, and `ADR-nnnn` IDs. A link that does not resolve as written is matched by its tail, so an archived item's links, written from where the item used to be, still resolve.
- The corpus is `design/system`, `design/tech`, and `design/adrs`, leaving out each folder's `README.md` and the ADR template. `docs/` is not in it: ADR-0047 names design, tech, and ADRs.
- A superseded ADR is replaced wherever it is chosen (by topics, links, or the step), following `superseded_by` to the ADR in force. The replacement's reason names the ADR it directly supersedes. Superseded ADRs are not ranked, and the catalog marks them.
- An ADR is chosen whole whichever of its sections matched. A design section chosen by ranking is one section down to the next heading of any level. A ranked ADR scores by its best section.
- Stopwords are dropped from the ranking query in the context package, not in `flai/internal/search`, so the dashboard's search is unchanged.
- `flai prime --story` accepts an archived story, printing the pack it would get today. The replay criterion needs this, and it reverses S-0136's refusal of archived stories, which is a refinement.
- The replay notes give each run's size and selection rather than the 500 KB outputs themselves.
