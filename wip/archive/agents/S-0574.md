## Decisions

- Scope, from an explorer's survey of every flaiover form seeded from server data: two forms revert what the user typed. `StrategicSettings.svelte` (the planner, orchestrator, analyzer, and role pages) copies each new read of the settings into its form, and the pages read them again on a `system-flow.yaml` change and on every live-events reconnect (`askAgain` in `lib/events.ts`). `SettingsPanel.svelte` rewrites every section's fields after a save in any one section. The other forms are seeded once, keyed, or never refilled on a push, so they are left as they are.
- Fix: one pure helper, `rebase(seed, current, fresh)` in `lib/form.ts`, keeps each field the user changed from what it was filled with and takes the rest from the new read. One rule for both forms rather than a dirty flag per form.
- Plan: layer 1 is T-2463 (the helper, its tests, the design line). Layer 2 is T-2464 (StrategicSettings) and T-2465 (SettingsPanel). Both wait for T-2463 because they import `rebase`. They share no path, so they run together.
