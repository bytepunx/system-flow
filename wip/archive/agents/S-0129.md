## Decisions

- The hold is read from `agent.status` whether or not the `agent` action is on (`storyActivity`), since flai reports holds either way; the board's own `held` field is not used, so the dot, the line, and the page come from one answer and clear together.
- The card names the story it waits for without a link: the card is one link and cannot hold another. The page links every story ID in the reason.
- The IDs a hold waits for are read from the reason's `starts when` clause, since flai's hold has no list of them; a structured field would touch `flai/`, outside this story's claim.
- Start agent stays offered for a held story that never had an agent, and Retry for a held story whose agent failed, because both override a hold on the host (ADR-0046). S-0128 had hidden both on the page by giving held stories an activity.
- StoryAgent tells the page the hold through an `onhold` callback rather than a bindable prop, which the lint reads as a useless assignment.
