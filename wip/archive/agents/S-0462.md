## Decisions

- Layers: 1 T-1969 (the open-lanes state); 2 T-1970 (the layout, which reads that state); 3 T-1971 and T-1972 together (the Playwright test drives the layout and the docs describe it, so both wait for T-1970; they share no path).
- T-1970 (task sub-agent): one markup for both widths. The lanes are a flex column below `sm` and a non-wrapping row from `sm` in an `overflow-x-auto` container; each lane is `sm:min-w-56 sm:flex-1` and scrolls its own cards under a sticky `<h2>`. A closed lane's card list is `hidden sm:block`. The layout drops `max-w-6xl` on `/board` only.
- The merge queue and the publish banner stay outside the collapsible card list, so a closed Review or Done lane still shows them: the criterion collapses the lane's cards, and Done starts closed on a phone, where hiding the banner would hide work waiting to be released.
- From `sm` a heading reads `aria-expanded` true and its click toggles nothing (a `matchMedia('(min-width: 40rem)')` state), so the narrow choice is used only below `sm` (criterion 3) and a screen reader never hears a visible lane called collapsed. Added after reviewing the sub-agent's work.
- No scroll snap on the row: it could fight the browser's auto-scroll while a card is dragged across lanes.
