## Decisions

- The cause of I-0301 is a window in the checked creation, not a start path that skips the gate. `itemnew.Create` writes a new item, checks it, and removes it when the check refuses it. flai serve's look at 2026-11-07T08:18:46Z read planner-S-0568's task T-2550 in that window, so S-0568's claim was that task's touches and no hold applied. T-2786's Notes give the evidence.
- The fix: a lock of its own, `.flai-cache/items-checked.lock`, held by a checked creation from its write until it keeps or removes the item, under which the look reads its items. It is apart from the items lock because `Save`, which the creation calls, takes that one, and a second flock on it would wait on itself. The planner's "judge the hold again at the start" would not fix it: a read moments later still sees the task. Recorded as an ADR in T-2789, since it is a contract between two processes.
- Checked edits of an existing item (`item_edit`, `flai touches`) have the same write-then-restore window. They are left out: no instance shows them, and the issue names a creation. Recorded as I-0328. ADR-0272 records the fix and the alternatives.
- T-2786's look marks the item folders changed under the lock (`workitem.ItemsChanged`). Otherwise a snapshot kept before the removal could still read as fresh until the watcher marks it. The cost is a stat of the active item files once a minute.
