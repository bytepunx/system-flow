- Deploy also shows for a story in review whose deploy at its head failed or was interrupted, beside one not deployed or deployed at an older commit, so a failed deploy can be tried again (T-2491).
- The page lists its rows as cards rather than a table, since each holds steps, logs, and demo commands (T-2491).
