## Decisions

- **Section rule, ADR-0217.** A reason pack keeps a convention section whose topics are `all` alone, and one tagged `all` and other words only when a word is a reason kind of the run, or `orchestration`. `all` keeps every tagged section in every other pack as before, so no story's, planner's, analyzer's, or curator's pack loses a rule.
  - Topics without `all` were rejected: they would narrow `work-management.md` for every story's pack.
  - A second `reasons:` heading comment was rejected: every heading reader would have to strip it.
- **Reason kinds** are the planner's: `thread:TH-nnnn`, `review:S-nnnn`, `planned:E|S-nnnn`, `draft:S-nnnn`, `release`, `ready`.
  - The trigger's events map onto them in flai serve (T-2313), not in the parser.
  - `asked` and `schedule` have no form, and a run with one primes with the general pack. This departs from the planner's mapping onto `ready` and `release`. Such a run looks at the whole project, threads and reviews included, which `ready` and `release` alone would not load.
- **The words go into `flai check`'s vocabulary** (T-2337), so the template's baseline checks clean in a new project. `check.go` is S-0418's claim too. On MS-0232, S-0418's agent agreed that S-0537 changes it first.
