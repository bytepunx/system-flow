# S-0200 An epic follows its stories: to ready and in-progress with the first, to review and done with the last

## Context

An epic's status is moved by hand today. The designer decided on 2026-10-02 that it follows its stories. Every move outside acceptance goes through `workitem.Repo.TransitionAll`: `flai move`, the MCP `item_move`, the dashboard's `item.move`, which runs `flai move`, and cancellation. Acceptance (`flai accept`, `flai move <story> done`, the dashboard's accept) calls `Repo.Move` itself in `cmd/accept.go`.

## Decisions
- Plan: layer 1 is T-0745, the rule and its API in `flai/internal/workitem`. Layer 2 is T-0748 (`flai/cmd/move.go`, `flai/cmd/accept.go`, `flai/internal/preview`), T-0749 (`flai/internal/mcpserver`, `flai/internal/hostapi`), and T-0750 (`flai/internal/check`): each waits for T-0745 because it calls its API, and they share no path, so they run together. Layer 3 is T-0752, the documents, which waits for the three so that it describes what they built.
- The epic's state is derived from its stories, cancelled ones left out: done when all are done, review when all are in review or done, in-progress when any has started (in-progress, review, or done), ready when any is ready, else backlog. It moves only in the direction its story moved: forward when the story moved forward or was cancelled, back when the story moved back or came back from cancelled. That is what the criteria say ("first story", "last open story", "only when no other story holds it"), and it never moves an epic against its story, so an epic someone moved ahead of its stories by hand is not dragged back by a forward move.
- The epic walks the allowed transitions one at a time (backlog to ready to in-progress), each with the story's actor and time and a note naming the story, as a cancellation cascade does (ADR-0028). It goes to done only in acceptance; a cancelled story whose siblings are all done takes the epic to review at most, for the operator to accept.
- No command creates a story in ready: every story is created in backlog (`flai story new`, MCP `item_new`, the dashboard's `item.new`). The rule hooks a story's entry into ready, which is the only way in, so "created in ready" needs nothing of its own.
- The inbox names the story an epic followed from the transitions alone, as `CancelledWith` does for a cascade: a child story's transition with the same time and actor. The front matter's transition keeps `to`, `at`, `by`; no field is added.
- A story given another epic (`flai edit --parent`) moves both epics: the one it joined as though it entered from backlog, the one it left as though it was cancelled out of it. Found when T-0750's warning made the edit's own check refuse such an edit (`TestEditFieldsParentAndBody`); `flai/internal/itemedit` is in the story's touches for this.
- Acceptance archives the epic with its last story and the epic's cancelled stories, because `PlanArchive` refuses an epic with stories still on the board (T-0748's sub-agent's choice, kept).
- The release tests' fixture (`pendingProject`) gets a third story in backlog, so that accepting its two stories does not accept E-0001 and the tests stay about stories' bumps.
- ADR renumbered to 0076: S-0207 took 0075 on its branch first (TH-0084, I-0062).
- TH-0082, answered by alex on 2026-10-03: option 2. An accepted epic contributes no release bump of its own; its stories carry theirs. This changes git.md's baseline rule ("an epic contributes a major bump") and `release.LevelFor`, and the documentation is updated to say so.
- T-0759 waits for T-0752, whose documents it corrects. I worked it myself rather than through a sub-agent: one function, two tests, and text in seven files, which I already held in context.
- Sync after S-0207's acceptance: the README, flai-cli.md, and summary conflicts were resolved by keeping both sides. My I-0057 bump commit was dropped and redone with `flai issue bump`, so its count and averages stay right. My own I-0062 duplicated S-0201's I-0062 on main, so it was removed and became an instance of that issue.
- The second ADR is ADR-0078: story/S-0201 already held 0077 (TH-0085).
- `epic.lags-stories` is advisory: an epic is the operator's to move, not a story's agent's, so it must not stop every close-out (as S-0243 did for the review limit).
