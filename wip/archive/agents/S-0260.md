## Context

I-0071: `item_edit` refused `.claude/agents/planner.md` as a touch, while `flai touches` set it. `itemedit.cleanList` and `hostapi`'s `listValue` check touches with the tags' pattern, which wants a letter or digit first; `flai touches` and `flai story new --touches` check nothing.

## Decisions

- The issue recommended no solution; the one proposed and built: one rule for a touch, `workitem.CleanTouches`, applied by every writer (`flai edit`, MCP `item_edit`, `flai touches`, `flai story new` and `flai task new`, the host API). A touch may start with a dot, as `.claude` and `.github` paths do, 25 times across `wip` already; it may not hold a comma (it would split in the front matter), start with a dash (it would read as a flag), be absolute, hold a `..` segment (it would name a path outside the repository), or hold a control character. Tags keep their own, stricter rule. Making `flai touches` and `new` refuse what they accepted before is the price of one rule; nothing in `wip` today breaks it.
- Plan: two layers. T-0865 (the code) waits for nothing; T-0866 (the design paragraph and closing I-0071) waits for T-0865, whose rule it records. No layer holds two tasks, so nothing runs together.
- T-0866 was worked by me, not a task sub-agent: one paragraph of design and `flai issue close`, which a sub-agent may not run. Its touches grew to `docs/users/flai.md`, since the rule is user-facing; the story's touches grew with it.
- In review of T-0865, `itemedit.cleanList` was renamed `cleanTags`: with touches gone to `CleanTouches` it has one caller, and `unparam` would flag its `what` parameter.
