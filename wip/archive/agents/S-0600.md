- The planner's plan (TH-0901) is taken as drafted: six tasks in three layers. Two tracks share no path, items (`flai/internal/workitem`) and lint (`flai/internal/mdlint`, `check.go`).
  - Layer 1: T-2674 (items pool, `store.go`) and T-2678 (folder lint pool, `folder.go`, `check.go`) wait for nothing and share no path, so they run together.
  - Layer 2: T-2682 (kept items) waits for T-2674 because both change `store.list`. T-2685 (kept lint findings) waits for T-2678 because both change `folder.go` and `check.go`. The two share no path, so they run together.
  - Layer 3: T-2686 (design and runbook) and T-2688 (measurement) wait for T-2682 and T-2685. They describe and time what those tasks build. They share no path.
- The planner's assumptions stand: the pool is `GOMAXPROCS` capped at 16 with no setting; the lint key hashes the configuration's bytes and a rule-set version; kept items restore `Path` and `Archived` and recompute `readAs`; the kept file is written only into a `.flai-cache` that exists, as S-0531 decided (D-0402).
- What the task sub-agents chose, reviewed and kept:
  - T-2674: stats run under the folder's lock and only parses are pooled, so lists at once still share one parse.
  - T-2678: `mdlint.LintFolder` returns only the files with findings, in walk order.
  - T-2682: one kept file per item folder, `.flai-cache/items-<folder>.json`, so a change to the active stories never rewrites the archive's entries. Items are kept as JSON, and `readAs` is recomputed with `Marshal`. `transition.go` was left alone, because its second list costs only stats in a process that has just listed.
  - T-2685: the kept file is `.flai-cache/lint-findings-<hash of folder>.json`, so a worktree and the main checkout never share one. The configuration's hash covers its bytes, its rules, `RulesVersion`, and the format version.
- T-2682 and T-2685 also changed files outside their touches: `flai/internal/workitem/repo.go` (one call), `flai/internal/check/check_test.go`, and the `flai/cmd` tests `promote_test.go` and `verify_test.go`. Those tests now skip the kept files through `keptParse`. They were committed with T-2682.
- Criterion 3 is met by the wall and CPU figures in `server-performance.md`. The title's 0.3 s for a cold list is not met at the median (0.94 s under a load average near 50). That is recorded as I-0310, not fixed here: the criteria do not ask for it.
