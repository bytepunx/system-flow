## Decisions

- **The plan's layers.** Layer 1: T-1174 (the ADR) and T-1176 (the permission, the candidates, and flai's own checks), which share no path. Layer 2: T-1177 (guard), T-1178 (prompt, definition, convention), and T-1179 (design and guides). Each waits on T-1176 for `PermitPlanBacklogStories` and the texts it settles. They share no path and run together. T-1178's harness edits could briefly break the `cmd` package T-1177 tests, so each sub-agent is told to wait and retry such a failure.
- **What "missing a plan" means.** A backlog story lacking touches, a cost of delay value, or a task that is not cancelled, as criterion 2 names them, plus a forecast duration. The planner sets the forecast with the touches, and `flai promote --candidates` needs one, so a story without one cannot move on.
- **No planning loop.** A story whose newest planner run ended, and which has not changed since, is left out of the candidates. A planner that could not finish is not started again until something changes, such as the orchestrator setting the cost of delay the planner asked for.
- **Scope of the threads.** Only threads a story's planner opened (`planner-S-nnnn`). An epic planner's threads stay under `answer_threads`, since the permission is about stories. "Accept the planner's recommendation" means setting the figure it recommends and replying. Confirming a recommendation entry stays the operator's (ADR-0090).
- **Cost of delay edits.** The orchestrator may add the cost of delay inputs of a backlog story whose story and epic have none, and nothing else of the story: no value, no clear, no other field. This narrows ADR-0119 §4, which says it may set the inputs and the value. The narrowing follows the planner's T-1172: it never overwrites the operator's inputs, and the value stays a figure flai and the planner work out. flai holds this as well as the guard, as it does for the other permissions.
- **The planner's tasks cancelled.** The operator asked for S-0328 to be planned ten seconds after this agent started it. The planner drafted T-1168 to T-1173 and T-1175 while T-1174 and T-1176 to T-1179 were being written. Its design matched this one. Its tasks are cancelled as duplicates, and two of its refinements are folded in: the candidates' JSON names each one's type, and the cost of delay rule above. Its tasks also named `TestTheOrchestratorNeverPlansAStoryEditsAnItemOrPlacesOneByHand`, now in T-1177. T-1176 also touches `cmd/orchestrate_permissions_test.go`, where flai's own refusals change, so T-1177 waits on it.
