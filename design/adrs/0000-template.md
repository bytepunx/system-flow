# ADR-0000 Title

## Context

What situation forces a decision. Facts, constraints, and the question being answered.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense. One decision. If two things were decided, write two ADRs.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what must now be done, and what was given up.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative with one line on why it lost.
