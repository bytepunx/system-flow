## Finding 1: a story in review whose close-out fails after main moved waits for the operator, and its `after` chain starves the board

**Severity: high.** Issue: [I-0386](../issues/I-0386-a-story-in-review-whose-close-out-fails-after-main-moved-waits-for-the-operator.md).
