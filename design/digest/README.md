# digest

What past stories learned, one fact per file, so that a later story's agent is primed with it rather than asking again. An entry is a decision from a narrative or an ADR, the operator's answer on a thread, a recommendation the operator confirmed or overruled, or an issue's cause and fix. It is named `D-nnnn-<slug>.md`, its ID and its title's slug. Its front matter has `id`, `title` (the fact, as one sentence), `kind` (`decision`, `answer`, `recommendation`, or `cause`), `sources` (the work items, threads, and issues it comes from), `topics`, `paths`, `date`, `status` (`active`, `absorbed` once a document records it, with `absorbed_by` naming that document, or `compacted`), `asked_again`, and `cited`. Below `# D-nnnn <title>`, a few sentences say what was asked or decided, the answer and its reason, and link the sources. [ADR-0191](../adrs/0191-the-digest-is-one-markdown-file-per-entry-under-the-design-folder-s-digest.md) gives each key.

Only flai and the digest's keeper, the curator, write here, and only through `flai digest new` and `flai digest edit`, which number the entry, write its front matter, and refuse a write the check finds a problem in. This folder has no index: `flai digest list` lists the entries, and `flai digest show <D-nnnn>` prints one. An entry says where a secret lives, never its value; `flai check` reports `digest.secret` on text with the shape of a key or token, and `digest.entry` on an entry whose name or front matter is wrong. Nothing here is deleted: an entry is absorbed or compacted by a change of its status, and git keeps every earlier text.

`flai digest seed` filled this folder first, in S-0429, with an entry for each answer the operator gave on a thread and each closed issue's cause and fix, as `flai digest harvest` lists them. Those entries are a starting point, not the digest's final word: each quotes its source as it was written, terse answers included, and the curator merges, retitles, absorbs, or compacts them as it takes in the archive's decisions.
